## Backstory

Perrin Black-Jaw grew up in the Tangle, the family network at the heart of the Warren beneath Calveno. He built a life methodically: contacts, capital, crew, a ship — the _Vestra_, inherited from his grandfather. He loved the fishing. Then something vast came up from below and the hull was simply gone.

He survived on the Cloak of the Manta Ray. He washed up at Keth-Naar. Something enormous and otherworldly found him in the water afterward and decided he was worth speaking to. He is still working out what that means.

He is aboard the _Saltwright_ now, in the hold, staying in motion — because the alternative is calling Nona and explaining what happened to her late husband's ship.
