# Fighter

_Class — Player's Handbook (2024)_

**Primary Ability:** Strength or Dexterity · **Hit Die:** d10 · **Saves:** Strength, Constitution · **Armor:** All armor + Shields · **Weapons:** Simple and Martial

---

## Core Features

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Fighting Style (feat choice), Second Wind (2/SR), Weapon Mastery (3 weapons) |
| 2 | Action Surge (1/SR), Tactical Mind |
| 3 | Subclass |
| 5 | Extra Attack (×2), Tactical Shift |
| 9 | Indomitable (1/LR), Tactical Master |
| 11 | Two Extra Attacks (×3 total) |
| 13 | Studied Attacks, Indomitable (2/LR) |
| 17 | Action Surge (2/SR), Indomitable (3/LR) |
| 20 | Three Extra Attacks (×4 total) |

---

## Feature Summaries

**Second Wind.** Bonus action: regain 1d10 + Fighter level HP. Uses scale with level (2→3→4). Recharge: 1 on Short Rest, all on Long Rest.

**Weapon Mastery.** Use mastery properties on 3 weapon types (scales to 6 at level 16). Swap one choice each Long Rest.

**Action Surge.** Take one extra action on your turn (not Magic action). 1/SR; 2/SR at level 17.

**Tactical Mind.** On a failed ability check, expend a Second Wind use to add 1d10 to the roll (use not spent if still fails).

**Tactical Shift.** When you use Second Wind, move up to half your speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Extra Attack.** Attack twice per Attack action (L5); three times (L11); four times (L20).

**Indomitable.** Reroll a failed saving throw, adding your Fighter level as a bonus. Must use new roll. 1/LR (scales to 3/LR).

**Tactical Master.** When attacking with a mastered weapon, replace its mastery property with Push, Sap, or Slow.

**Studied Attacks.** Miss an attack roll → Advantage on your next attack vs. same creature before end of next turn.
