# Cat's Curios

A narrow door in a [[places/Calveno|Calveno]] side-street — brass fittings, no name. Inside: long benches of half-finished mechanisms, a low forge, the smell of hot oil and soldering flux, and a locked glass cabinet along one wall holding pieces that don't belong to this century.

The workshop belongs to [[npcs/Catarina-DaVirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]. She does not advertise. She does not take walk-ins. Finding her requires a referral from someone she trusts, and she does not expand that circle casually.
