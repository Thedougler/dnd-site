# Canal Boat Hire

A flat-bottomed canal boat, poled or rowed, suitable for navigating [[Calveno]]'s canal system. Available for half-day or full-day hire at the [[Ponte-Bassa|Ponte Bassa]]. [[Oleandro-Fuschi|Oleandro]] does not ask where you are going. Two boats available; deposit required.
