# Meth-Var

> _The island that watches the water no one trusts._

The easternmost of the five Sunken Crown islands, facing open Maw waters on an exposed, windswept approach with little shelter for larger vessels. Meth-Var is not inhabited permanently. The people of [[places/Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] rotate watchers through it because the island's value is attention — it faces the direction where weather and deep movement can reach the Blue Hole before anyone in the central settlement sees the sign.

Watchers leave marks in paired sets: one for what was seen, one for who must be told. A sighting without a carried warning is considered unfinished work.

Lately, the warning patterns have been arriving wrong.
