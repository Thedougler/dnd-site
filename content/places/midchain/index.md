# The Midchain

The Midchain is the southern arc of the [[places/Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]]: close-packed limestone and coral islands, reef channels, free ports, local councils, back routes, Grung-held interiors, and charts that lose arguments to pilots.

## Region Overview

- [[Midchain|The Midchain]] — public gazetteer entry for the region as a whole.
- [[places/Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] — wider archipelago of ports, reefs, colonial claims, and old ruins.
- [[places/Central-Strait|The Central Strait]] — main east-west shipping corridor north of the Midchain.
- [[Doldrums|The Doldrums]] — mirror-flat dead water along the Midchain's southern edge.
- [[Tail|The Tail]] — eastern convergence of the Scatter near the [[places/Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]].

## Kalowe And Independent Waters

Kalowe is the Midchain's main free-port anchor: repair harbour, council meeting place, dry dock, and the place where ships with inconvenient papers can become a practical problem instead of an official one.

- [[Kalowe|Kalowe]] — primary Midchain free port and repair harbour.
- [[Chain-Council|The Chain Council]] — occasional assembly of independent Midchain representatives.
- [[ships/The-Bad-Receipt|The Bad Receipt]] — Kalowe-linked schooner and local cautionary name.
- [[ships/Knifes-Wake|Knife's Wake]] — small raider suited to the Midchain's tight channels.
- [[ships/The-Glass-Debt|The Glass Debt]] — name sailors connect with increasingly expensive convoy routes.

## Verdant Teeth

The Verdant Teeth are the Grung-held island cluster south of Kalowe. Outsiders trade at controlled beaches, stay at the waterline, and treat the interiors as closed.

- [[Verdant-Teeth|The Verdant Teeth]] — five reef-fringed Grung islands with strict trade protocols and lethal boundaries.
- [[Veth|Veth]] — largest island and only sanctioned trade contact point with the [[factions/Grung-Clans|Grung clans]].
- [[Sorn|Sorn]] — agricultural interior island with guarded beach traffic.
- [[Karath|Karath]] — hostile Verdant Teeth island where reef-gap approaches are met with dart fire.
- [[Dreth|Dreth]] — eastern Verdant Teeth island, low, wet, quieter than Karath, and still Grung territory.
- [[Orak|Orak]] — eastern support island paired with Dreth across a narrow shadowed channel.
- [[Aruhe|Aruhe]] — deserted island east of Karath, abandoned after Grung pressure and now avoided by pilots.

## Rumours And Eastern Reefs

- [[Halythion|Halythion]] — rumoured underwater city somewhere in the eastern reefs south of the Midchain.
- [[places/Antheri-Ruins|Antheri Ruins]] — ancient deep-water structures associated with the Maw and the Shelfworks.
- [[places/Shelfworks|The Shelfworks]] — salvage field on the Maw's western shelf.

## Passage Notes

Midchain passage is local before it is official. Reefs shift, channels silt, storms open and close back routes, and a pilot who knows the water is worth more than a clean chart.

- Back channels can help westbound ships avoid predictable Strait inspection routes.
- Deep blue holes are avoided unless a pilot knows them by name.
- The Doldrums shift by season, making southern route planning a moving problem.
- The Verdant Teeth trade route is profitable, narrow, and watched.

## Hazards

- [[beastiary/Sawek|Sawek]] — apex sinkhole predators whose lairs can look like useful shelter from above.
- [[beastiary/giant-crocodile|Giant crocodiles]] — mangrove cuts and river mouths.
- [[beastiary/giant-constrictor-snake|Giant constrictor snakes]] — flooded channels and rainforest margins.
- [[beastiary/giant-frog|Giant frogs]] — flooded cuts, rain pools, and abandoned terraces.
- [[beastiary/giant-spider|Giant spiders]] — high canopy, abandoned brush, and Verdant Teeth interiors.
- [[beastiary/giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]] — warm limestone, terrace walls, and reef-island brush.
- [[beastiary/giant-wasp|Giant wasps]] — crane works, roof beams, and rainforest canopies.
- [[beastiary/pirate|Pirates]] — crews using local channels, false papers, and becalmed ships as opportunity.
