## Backstory

Jean-Claude grew up in the Botukuri clan of the Verdant Teeth, in a society built on slavery and caste. He operated as a scout — acquiring slaves, developing ambush tactics — with the fervor of someone chasing clan glory, blind to the suffering he caused. He was good at it.

A gnome slave named Pell changed him. Conversations stolen between chores. The idea that every people has something the others lack. That the world outside was larger, stranger, and less inevitable than the clan made it sound.

His plan to free Pell was discovered by his sister Simone. He walked to his parents' hut on what felt like the best day of his life and found Pell already in chains. What followed was a month of torture, conducted specifically for Jean-Claude's benefit. When he finally ran, he freed every slave he could reach. They were slaughtered in the chaos. Their screams covered his exit.

He has been moving since. He is not ready to go back yet. He knows this. He intends to be ready eventually.
