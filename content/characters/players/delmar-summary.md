# Delmar Atticus Fisk

> _Youngest Admiral in living memory. Nobody official gave him the title — he just wore it long enough that it became true._

## Overview

**Delmar Atticus Fisk**, commonly called **Admiral Fisk**, is a human admiral, captain, and privateer associated with the lost five-ship force known as Fisk's Fleet. By the opening of the Shattered Sea campaign, he is forty-one years old and counted among the two living witnesses to the recent destruction of the _Red Lady_ near the [[places/The-Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]].

Fisk's rank is a matter of reputation more than paperwork. He is widely remembered as the youngest admiral in living memory, though no recognized navy is known to have formally granted him the title before he began wearing it. In the ports of the Shattered Sea, where authority often follows usefulness, the distinction has rarely stopped anyone from using it.

## Appearance

Fisk is immediately recognizable: sandy red hair tied in a messy topknot, a thick beard, a curled mustache going grey at the edges, and a long red admiral's coat with gold epaulettes. Accounts of him tend to mention the same detail first: he smiles with his eyes before his mouth catches up.

He is commonly seen with two named weapons, **the Baroness**, a rapier, and **the Duchess**, a musket. Both have become part of the public image around him, the kind of details that survive retelling even when the rest of a dockside story changes shape.

## Career

Fisk made his name outside formal naval service. He is associated with independent sailing, privateering, salvage work, and negotiations where a steady smile can matter as much as a drawn blade. His public reputation rests on confidence under pressure, loyalty to his crew, and a habit of treating survival as something that can usually be talked into happening.

At his height, Fisk commanded a five-ship fleet led by the _Red Lady_. The fleet was considered fast, competent, and unusually self-contained, built for long operations away from easy port support and crewed by sailors who had reason to trust the admiral's judgment.

## The Red Lady Disaster

Shortly before the campaign begins, Fisk's Fleet was destroyed near the Drowned Maw. The _Red Lady_ and her accompanying vessels went down in the same catastrophe. The details remain difficult to verify, as is common with Maw-adjacent wrecks and uncommon with disasters that leave so few survivors.

Fisk survived. So did [[crissdalynn-summary|Crisdalynn Khinriss]], the aarakocra scout credited with pulling him clear as the ships went under. Two days later, [[lore/Saltwright|the *Saltwright*]] found them among wreckage and took them aboard.

Among sailors, the disaster has already begun to harden into legend: the admiral in the red coat, the aarakocra diving through impossible water, the fleet vanishing east of the charts, and the survivors carried west on an ordinary merchant brig.

## Character

Fisk is a talker by instinct and a fighter by necessity. He prefers to talk his way out of trouble when possible; when he cannot, the rapier and musket come out in that order. Those who have seen him under pressure describe quick assessment, quick action, and little wasted motion.

His loyalty is strongest toward found family and crew. The loss of the _Red Lady_ and her captains is not, for Fisk, a closed chapter. It remains the central fact of his present life: a command lost, friends gone, and unfinished obligations still pulling him east.

## Key Relationships

- **[[crissdalynn-summary|Crisdalynn Khinriss]]** — fellow survivor of the _Red Lady_ disaster; credited with saving Fisk's life.
- **[[Fisks-Fleet|Fisk's Fleet]]** — the destroyed five-ship force formerly under his command.
- **The _Red Lady_** — Fisk's flagship, lost near the Drowned Maw.
- **[[lore/Saltwright|The *Saltwright*]]** — merchant brig that rescued Fisk and Crisdalynn after the wreck.

## Public Standing

Fisk's reputation depends heavily on who is speaking. To some, he is an admiral in everything but paperwork: daring, loyal, and difficult to kill. To others, he is a privateer with expensive habits, a dangerous name, and a fleet-sized tragedy in his wake.

What is not disputed is that Delmar Fisk is alive, travelling aboard the _Saltwright_, and still carrying the weight of the ships that did not come back.
