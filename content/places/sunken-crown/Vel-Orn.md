# Vel-Orn — Sunken Crown

Vel-Orn is the barren black-stone island that holds [[Umberlee-Shrine|Umberlee's shrine]] in the [[Sunken-Crown|Sunken Crown]]. The name carries the local tabaxi sense of water-debt. Outsiders and hurried guides still call it Shrine Island.

The people of [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] treat Vel-Orn as a boundary, not a destination. It is visible from the Lookout Stone as a black point across the water, but visibility does not make it welcoming. The island has no natural harbour, no village, no hunting ground, and no refuge worth seeking.

Its cultural role is debt and restraint. The living do not go there for ordinary reasons. It is where offerings pass into Umberlee's account, where the drowned are remembered, and where visitors are expected to behave as if the sea is listening.

---

## Approach

There is no dock, no welcome fire, and no beach worth naming. A skiff has to find a break in the stone and time the surge. The rock is sharp, black, and exposed, with pale reef around it and deep water close by.

Keth-Naar guides do not speak loudly on the crossing. They keep their hands visible, leave hooks covered, and do not bring fresh blood onto the rock.

Vel-Orn has few animals because there is little to eat, which makes any sign of life stand out. [[beastiary/giant-bat|Giant bats]] sometimes cling under the black overhangs, and [[beastiary/swarm-of-ravens|ravens]] gather after offerings or wreckage wash ashore. Local guides treat both as practical hazards and bad company around a place already heavy with debt.
