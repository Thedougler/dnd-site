# Beasts

Natural predators, swarms, and wildlife of the Scatter's islands and waters.

## Large Predators

- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-crocodile|Giant Crocodile]] - Apex ambush predator of the tidal rivers and mangrove channels of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-constrictor-snake|Giant Constrictor Snake]] - Found in rainforest interiors of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] and [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]]; large enough to pin and suffocate a horse
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-ape|Giant Ape]] - Territorial apex predator of dense rainforest interiors; sightings at [[Sorn|Sorn]] suggest at least one family controls the highland canopy
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-boar|Giant Boar]] - Charges without warning from undergrowth of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; big enough to stave in a ship's boat
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-shark|Giant Shark]] - Follows cold upwellings and blood trails; a serious threat near the [[Galewall|Galewall's]] wreckage and along [[places/tail/index|the Tail's]] longline grounds
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-squid|Giant Squid]] - Deep-water ambush predator that rises from the drop-off at night; a hazard for divers at the [[Shelfworks|Shelfworks]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/panther|Panther]] - Silent apex predator of the dense canopy of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]]; a party may be stalked for hours before it commits
- [[beastiary/beasts/tiger|Tiger]] - Forest and grassland predator; fast, powerful, and rarely seen before the first strike
- [[beastiary/beasts/owlbear|Owlbear]] - Ambush predator of the dense interior forests of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; the screech is usually the last thing heard before contact
- [[beastiary/beasts/primeval-owlbear|Primeval Owlbear]] - A massive prehistoric variant, CR 7; significantly stronger and more durable than its common cousin
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-axe-beak|Giant Axe Beak]] - Aggressive flightless predator on the clifftop scrublands of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] and exposed ridgelines; fast enough to run down a horse

## Birds

- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-eagle|Giant Eagle]] - Nests in the clifftop aeries of the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]]; large enough to carry a grown sailor
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-vulture|Giant Vulture]] - Carrion bird found wherever the sea deposits its dead; circles the clifftops of the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]] and the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-owl|Giant Owl]] - Nocturnal forest hunter of the old-growth interiors of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; near-silent on the wing
- [[beastiary/beasts/pegasus|Pegasus]] - Rumoured to range the updrafts above the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]]; the Sentinels claim sightings but treat the subject as a private matter
- [[beastiary/beasts/unicorn|Unicorn]] - Unconfirmed; a single account from a [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] interior expedition describes a white-maned creature that vanished before anyone else could see it

## Reptiles and Amphibians

- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-lizard|Giant Lizard]] - Rocky-shore and ruin predator of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]] and [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]]; territorial and fast
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-toad|Giant Toad]] - Ambush predator of boggy lowlands and freshwater margins of [[Karath|Karath]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-frog|Giant Frog]] - Marshland and tidal-flat ambush predator of [[Sorn|Sorn]], the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]], and [[Orak|Orak]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/constrictor-snake|Constrictor Snake]] - Common tree-and-water snake of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] lowlands; large enough to take a dog
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-venomous-snake|Giant Venomous Snake]] - Large ambush serpent of the mid-canopy and river margins in the [[Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]] and [[Karath|Karath]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/venomous-snake|Venomous Snake]] - Common bite hazard of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] undergrowth; small enough to go unnoticed until stepped on
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-seahorse|Giant Seahorse]] - Large enough to serve as a mount; found in sheltered reef waters of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]]

## Arthropods and Arachnids

- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-scorpion|Giant Scorpion]] - Found in dry rocky terrain of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]]; hides under flat stone and inside hollow logs
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-spider|Giant Spider]] - Web-builder of the deep jungle interior; found across the [[Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]], [[Karath|Karath]], and [[Orak|Orak]]
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-wolf-spider|Giant Wolf Spider]] - Hunting spider of the undergrowth of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] and [[Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]]; doesn't web, runs fast
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-wasp|Giant Wasp]] - Nests in cliff overhangs and hollow shoreline trees around [[The-Doldrums|the Doldrums]] and [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]; disturbing one nest sends the whole colony
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-centipede|Giant Centipede]] - Found under loose stone and damp timber throughout [[Sorn|Sorn]]; bites rather than charges
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-crab|Giant Crab]] - Intertidal heavyweight of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] beaches; large enough to shear rope
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-octopus|Giant Octopus]] - Reef-dwelling ambush predator in the rocky shallows of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]]

## Small Creatures and Pests

- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-bat|Giant Bat]] - Cave-roosting hunter of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]] and [[places/tail/index|the Tail]]; emerges at dusk in columns thick enough to blot the stars
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-rat|Giant Rat]] - Dock-bred pest of [[Kalowe|Kalowe's]] warehouses; larger than a cat and aggressive when cornered
- [[beastiary/beasts/giant-weasel|Giant Weasel]] - Burrow hunter of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] forest floor; occasionally found in cargo holds after a Crown Islands layover
- [[beastiary/beasts/boar|Boar]] - Common feral hog of the scrubland and coastal heath of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]

## Swarms

- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-bats|Swarm of Bats]] - Vast cave colony of the sea-cliff overhangs of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]]; the evening departure sounds like distant thunder
- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-rats|Swarm of Rats]] - Hull-rot hazard of [[Kalowe|Kalowe's]] waterfront; infests badly-kept stowage and spreads between vessels in harbour
- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-ravens|Swarm of Ravens]] - Carrion flock from the cliffs of [[Vel-Orn|Vel-Orn]]; circles over a vessel in distress is a universally understood bad sign
- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-insects|Swarm of Insects]] - Working hazard of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] jungle floor; biting clouds that can force a camp to move in under an hour
- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-piranhas|Swarm of Piranhas]] - Found in still interior lakes and spring-fed pools of [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] islands
- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-venomous-snakes|Swarm of Venomous Snakes]] - Disturbed nest or flooded burrow; the interior undergrowth harbours more than it shows
- [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-larvae|Swarm of Larvae]] - Linked to planar scarring near [[Antheri-Ruins|Antheri Ruins]]; larval forms of the damned that swarm toward the living
