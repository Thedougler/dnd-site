# Places in the Shattered Sea

## The Whole Region

- [[places/The-Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] - warm archipelago of reefs, ports, colonial claims, old ruins, sea tribute, and dangerous routes.

## Western Waters

- [[places/The-Galewall|The Galewall]] - permanent western storm belt separating the Scatter from the colonial homelands.
- [[places/Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]] - cold volcanic spire-chain near the Galewall.

## The Crown Islands

- [[places/Crown-Islands|The Crown Islands]] - northern arc of large, fortified islands where Dravosi authority is strongest.
- [[places/Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]] - western gatekeeper island of the Central Strait.
- [[places/Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]] - Dravosi fortress-port carved into Calder's Tooth.
- [[places/Fort-Crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] - Dravosi fort crowning Port Tidefall.
- [[places/South-Harbour|South Harbour]] - eastern working harbour of Calder's Tooth.
- [[places/Cape-Solitude|Cape Solitude]] - wind-battered northern point of Calder's Tooth.
- [[places/Calveno|Calveno]] - canal city of witnessed deals, pale stone, and carefully public commerce.

## The Central Strait

- [[places/Central-Strait|The Central Strait]] - main east-west shipping corridor between the Crown Islands and the Midchain.

## The Midchain

- [[places/The-Midchain|The Midchain]] - southern arc of close-packed islands, free ports, reefs, and local arrangements.
- [[places/Kalowe|Kalowe]] - main Midchain free port and repair harbour.
- [[places/Verdant-Teeth|The Verdant Teeth]] - Grung-held island cluster south of Kalowe.
- [[places/Sorn|Sorn]] - reef-fringed Grung island known from the water as green wall, pale sand, and danger.
- [[places/Karath|Karath]] - Verdant Teeth island with a hostile reputation and no visible invitation to land.

## The Tail And The Maw

- [[places/The-Tail|The Tail]] - eastern convergence of the Scatter near the Drowned Maw.
- [[places/The-Drowned-Maw|The Drowned Maw]] - vast eastern trench where charts, compasses, and sailors grow less confident.
- [[places/The-High-Eyrie|The High Eyrie]] - basalt sea-stack monastery of the Sentinels of the Eyrie.
- [[places/The-Shelfworks|The Shelfworks]] - open-water salvage field on the western shelf of the Drowned Maw.

## South of the Midchain

- [[places/The-Doldrums|The Doldrums]] - mirror-flat dead water where sound carries too well and wind forgets its work.

## Beyond The Maw

- [[places/Outer-Reach|The Outer Reach]] - open ocean beyond the Drowned Maw.
- [[places/Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] - tabaxi settlement island of Keth-Naar in the Sunken Crown.

## Hidden And Rumoured

- [[places/The-Warren|The Warren]] - rumours of the Rattkin place beneath Calveno that most Dry do not truly know.
