_Stub — created from fleet records. No dedicated source yet._

Captain of [[Loud-Argument-Secrets]], [[Fisks-Fleet-Secrets]]'s combat vessel. The fleet's dedicated combat specialist. ## Mortis Voice

**Roleplay Prompt:** Nicolas Cage as a pirate captain who prides himself on being impossible to ignore.

**Personality in Fisk's head:** Theatrical, confrontational, impossible to ignore. Made scenes as a leadership strategy — loud enough that nobody could pretend not to notice. Surprisingly effective at the big bluff, because it was always delivered with total conviction. When Bastian surfaces, Delmar goes all-in on whatever he is doing, commits harder than is wise, and cannot watch someone powerless get pushed around without making it everyone else's problem too.

**Forced trigger:** An enemy's Deception or Persuasion check succeeds against Delmar, or he witnesses someone powerless being openly coerced or threatened.

**Skills granted:** Performance, Deception _(Expertise — +7 while surfaced)_

**Roleplay cue:** Delmar gets louder and bigger. The charm becomes performance. He'll make a scene. He'll say the unfiltered thing at full volume. He'll bluff when bluffing is inadvisable, and he'll commit to it completely.
