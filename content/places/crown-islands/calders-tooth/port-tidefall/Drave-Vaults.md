# [[Drave-Vaults-Secrets|Drave Vaults]]

Crown architectural surveys of [[Fort-Crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] note several sealed sub-basements beneath the fort's foundations, recorded as structural errors from the original construction. They are not accessible. No further information is on public record.
