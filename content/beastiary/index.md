# Bestiary

The [[places/Shattered-Sea|Shattered Sea]] has things in it. Sailors name them, argue about them, and occasionally get killed by them. Some entries are confirmed creatures. Others are the kind of rumor that survives because the alternative explanation is worse.

**[[beastiary/Leviathan|The Leviathan]]** — Rumored deep-sea titan tied to the destruction of the _Vestra_. Perrin Black-Jaw survived the sinking, and his account gives the old boiling-water stories teeth. No reliable public statblock exists.

**[[beastiary/Sawek|Sawek (Blue Devil)]]** — An ambush predator that hunts the [[places/Midchain|Midchain]] blue holes. It waits in deep water, strikes with tentacles, and pulls prey down into its lair. Most people who've seen one up close didn't come back to describe it.

**[[beastiary/Whip-Shark|Whip Shark (Welak)]]** — Open-ocean hunter. Big enough to damage a ship, fast enough that you probably won't outrun it, and equipped with a barbed tail it uses like a whip. Stays deep unless something draws it up.
