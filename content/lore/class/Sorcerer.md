# Sorcerer

_Class — Player's Handbook (2024)_

**Primary Ability:** Charisma · **Hit Die:** d6 · **Saves:** Constitution, Charisma · **Armor:** None · **Weapons:** Simple · **Spellcasting:** CHA (prepared, up to 9th level, full caster)

---

## Sorcery Points

Start with 2 at Level 2; gain 1 per level (max = Sorcerer level). Fully recharge on Long Rest; partial recovery (half level, rounded down) once per Long Rest via Sorcerous Restoration (L5).

**Convert spell slots to points:** expend a slot, gain points equal to its level.

**Create spell slots (Bonus Action):**

| Slot Level | Cost | Min Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 SP | 2 |
| 2 | 3 SP | 3 |
| 3 | 5 SP | 5 |
| 4 | 6 SP | 7 |
| 5 | 7 SP | 9 |

Created slots vanish at Long Rest. Cannot create above level 5.

---

## Spell Slots

Full caster progression. 2 prepared spells at Level 1, scaling to 22 at Level 20. 4 cantrips at L1, 5 at L4, 6 at L10.

---

## Core Features

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Spellcasting (CHA), Innate Sorcery |
| 2 | Font of Magic (Sorcery Points), Metamagic (choose 2) |
| 3 | Subclass |
| 5 | Sorcerous Restoration |
| 7 | Sorcery Incarnate |
| 10 | Metamagic (2 more options) |
| 17 | Metamagic (2 more options) |
| 19 | Epic Boon |
| 20 | Arcane Apotheosis |

**Innate Sorcery (L1):** Bonus Action: +1 to spell save DC, Advantage on Sorcerer spell attack rolls for 1 minute. 2 uses per Long Rest; can spend 2 SP to activate when out of uses (L7 Sorcery Incarnate).

**Sorcery Incarnate (L7):** While Innate Sorcery is active, apply up to 2 Metamagic options per spell.

**Arcane Apotheosis (L20):** While Innate Sorcery is active, use one Metamagic option per turn for free (no SP cost).
