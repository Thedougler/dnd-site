# Savia Brentino

![[raw/assets/banners/Savia-Brentino.webp|Savia Brentino inspecting a hull at Carpenters' Slip near Calveno's Arsenal canal]]

## Summary

Compact and broad-shouldered, sawdust permanently in her eyebrows and probably her bloodstream. She speaks in structural assessments — "that transom's carrying too much" is hello; "she'll hold through the Strait" is as warm as she gets. She does not quote a timeline she cannot keep and does not do cosmetic work. Vessels that need to look like something else go to Acqua Nera; vessels that need to actually float go here.

## Role

Proprietor of [[Carpenters-Slip]], the fast-turnaround hull and mast yard the Arsenal won't prioritise. Takes jobs the official dry dock is too slow or too visible for.

## Relationships

- **[[Carpenters-Slip]]** — operates the slip
- **[[Calveno]]** — outer harbour, Arsenal-adjacent
