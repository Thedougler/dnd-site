# The Tail

![[raw/assets/banners/The-Tail.webp]]

## Overview

The Tail is where both arcs of the Scatter converge and narrow toward the [[places/The-Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]. The islands here are fewer, taller, and more vertical than anywhere else in the Sea — high cliffs dropping straight to cold deep water, morning fog burning off by midday, the Greyteeth reefs worst in the eastern approaches. A cold current wells up along the Maw's western edge and pushes nutrient-rich water across the shelf, which makes the Tail's longline grounds some of the best in the Scatter: swordfish, albacore, and oilfish close to the surface at night. Compass drift begins within five miles of the Maw's edge. Maw-adjacent navigation runs by stars, not instruments.

The communities here have lived next to the trench for generations. They know the shelf edge, they know the fog schedules, and they know where the Maw's discolouration starts on a clear day without needing to look hard. Longline crews work the grounds, salvage operations stage from the closest inhabited island before heading to the [[places/The-Shelfworks|Shelfworks]], and pilots here charge what the knowledge is worth. Weather, current, and depth change quickly. A pleasant morning can become instrument work by noon.
