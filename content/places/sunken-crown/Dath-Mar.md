# Dath-Mar

> _A hard place kept for hard hours._

The smallest of the five Sunken Crown islands. Its name preserves the local tabaxi sense of strengthened stone — a place of endurance rather than comfort. A few individuals live there year-round by choice. Most use it only when they need to.

[[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] hunters retreat here when a storm splits boats from home, when someone needs distance from the central settlement, or when a decision is too heavy to bring to the elders without sitting with it first. The island's value is not what it offers. It is that it can hold a person above the water when the rest of the Blue Hole turns against them.

---

## Shelter Cairns

Cairns maintained by whoever last used them mark the leeward approaches and the sheltered cut in the rock — accessible only at certain tide angles. Leaving a cairn worse than you found it is treated as a serious breach by the Keth-Naar, because the next person to arrive may not have the luxury of doing without it.

Lately the current reversals around the Blue Hole have been making the approach less predictable, and skiffs strand on the wrong side of the rock more often than they used to.

The island is too exposed for a deep jungle, but not too exposed for sheltering animals. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] sun against black stone after storms, and [[beastiary/beasts/giant-bat|giant bats]] use the higher cracks when weather pins them away from better roosts. Keth-Naar hunters do not treat either as supernatural. They treat them as one more reason to check a cairn before trusting it.
