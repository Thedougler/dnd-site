# Killer Whale

Killer whales work the cold lanes — [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] deep water, the storm-cold approaches to the [[Galewall|Galewall]], the upwelling edges where the water turns dark and the fish run rich. They move in pods and they hunt in pods, which is what makes them different from sharks. A shark is an event. A pod of killer whales is a situation that develops, and experienced open-water sailors distinguish between the two quickly.

[[Galewall|The Galewall]] accounts are the ones that travel. A pod will follow debris and survivors for hours — not always attacking, sometimes only circling — and the survivors who make it back describe the waiting as the worst part. A Galewall crossing that produced two lifeboats returned with three survivors from the second boat and none from the first. The second boat's crew reported that the pod investigated both equally. They could not explain why it went the way it went, and most pilots do not press them. ^\[inferred]
