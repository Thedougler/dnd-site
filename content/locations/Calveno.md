# Calveno

> _Everything here is visible. That is the point._

## Appearance

The city comes into view slowly — stone rising straight out of the water, four and five stories of carved pale facade facing the canals. There are no docks in the way you know them. Flat-bottomed boats move through channels cut between the buildings, and the sound that carries across the water is not the sound of a port but the sound of a thousand conversations happening at once, close together, in the open. The facades facing the canals are detailed, worked stone. The backs of the same buildings are bare and dark — nobody is meant to look at those.

The Mercatura runs straight from the harbour inward, boats pressed in from both sides, the air sharp with salt and spice. The Bridge at the main canal crossing is three tiers of stone arch, wide enough for two boats abreast — the lowest level loud and crowded, the upper levels quiet in a different way. In Le Paludi, the oldest district, the buildings are shorter and the foundations have settled unevenly into ground that was never fully solid. The channels narrow until two boats cannot pass without touching. The Velo Quarter sits on a separate island connected by two low bridges, its buildings painted in colours the rest of the city does not permit itself, lantern light spilling across the water at all hours.

## Lore Sheet

Everything in Calveno is visible. That is the point. The canal city runs on witnessed transactions — deals struck in public, information traded in the open, the right association seen by the right people at the right moment. Seven hereditary merchant families hold Council seats and have been deadlocked four-to-three on Tessarine terms for long enough that the three holdouts have started to look like the interesting ones. Beneath Le Paludi's oldest drainage channels, the only fixed Rattkin settlement in the Scatter has been running the Passage's anchor point since before the Seven Houses existed. The city above does not know the full shape of what is below it. The Tangle knows the full shape of what is above. Unusual money is currently moving through the broker network toward the Maw — enough that the Tangle has noticed, not yet enough that they know who is spending it or why.

## Notable Locations

- **The Mercatura** — Commercial artery running straight from the harbour gates inward. Boats press in from both sides, loaded and moving; the smell is salt and spice and the sharpness of coin changing hands in volume. The Tessarine factor building anchors the harbour end — a full story above everything around it, pale stone, Tessarine columns, no signage. It does not need any.

- **The Bridge** — Three-tier stone arch at the main canal crossing, wide enough for two boats abreast. The lowest level is noise: vendors, fried food, bodies pressing in both directions. The middle tier is shuttered shops and closed doors. The top tier is plain stone with no windows facing the water and a single narrow staircase that turns out of sight. Money-changing and intelligence at the top; you can hear voices up there, but you cannot make out the words.

- **The Arsenal** — Walled shipyard in the southwest, separated by a fortified canal. The smell of hot pitch and fresh-cut timber carries a full canal-length before the walls come into view. Masts in various stages of completion rise above the stonework; the overlapping rhythm of hammers runs without pause. Fourteen-month non-House wait. Operated by Orsola Grimani.

- **Le Paludi** — Oldest district; pre-city marsh channels; working-class population. The buildings here are shorter and older, their foundations settled unevenly into ground that was never fully solid. The channels narrow until two boats cannot pass without touching, and the water moves differently here — slower, darker, with a smell beneath the brine that belongs to something older than the city built above it. Secondary market with no Mercatura records; numbers spoken quietly, once, not repeated. The original drainage network runs beneath it.

- **The Velo Quarter** — Separate island attached by two low bridges. Lantern light spills across the water at all hours and music leaks from several directions at once, none of it the same song. The buildings are lower here, painted in colours the rest of Calveno does not permit itself. The air smells of warm wax and something sweeter underneath. Whatever authority governs the rest of the city stops at the bridges. Entertainment, gaming, pleasure houses; neutral ground where no House's authority fully applies.
