# Powers of the Shattered Sea

Every dock has a price. Every flag means something. Every claim of neutrality should be read twice.

These notes collect common public knowledge about the powers most often named in Shattered Sea ports.

- [[Dravosi-Crown-Summary|The Dravosi Crown]] - law, inspection, fortress harbours, naval force, and paperwork that can follow a ship across years.
- [[Tessarine-Concordat-Summary|The Tessarine Concordat]] - credit, contracts, insurance, route money, and debts that become politics.
- [[Waveservants-Summary|The Waveservants]] - Umberlee's grey-robed clergy, harbour shrines, tribute, and blessings given without warmth.
- [[Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie-Summary|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] - aarakocra observers of the Drowned Maw and keepers of the long record.
- [[The-Passage-Summary|The Passage]] - Rattkin Runs, Holds, courier routes, hidden spaces, and Kin memory beneath the ports.
- [[Grung-Clans-Summary|The Grung]] - Verdant Teeth clans, colour-caste protocol, waterline trade, poison, bondage, and worsening raids.
