## What the Party Might Know

Shepherd Grigori is a miracle worker. That's the story — a holy man who appeared from nowhere and started curing noble heirs of things that shouldn't be curable. Red and gold coat, glass of wine, always a guest on someone else's ship.

He's supposed to be impossible to kill. Poison. Drowning. Worse. He survives. The nobility who've benefited from his cures don't tend to ask how.
