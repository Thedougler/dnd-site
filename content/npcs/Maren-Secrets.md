# [[Maren]]

## Mortis Voice

**Roleplay Prompt:** Regina Hall as a pirate captain — warm, direct, and she will correct you on her name before she does anything else.

**Personality in Fisk's head:** Loud where Delmar is smooth. Sassy where he is charming. When Maren surfaces, the performance drops and the opinions come out — about the Navy, about whoever is standing in the way, about the fact that there is always another door if you know where to look. She is completely comfortable in crowds and chaos; large groups do not register as a threat to someone raised in a house of twelve. She corrects her name. Every time, without hesitation.

**Forced trigger:** Delmar is denied entry or passage is physically blocked — a locked door, a guard refusing to let him through, a sealed gate. Maren surfaces and starts looking for the other way in.

**Environmental trigger:** A Navy vessel or Navy insignia clearly in view, or a Navy officer identified within earshot. Maren surfaces. The contempt is immediate and visible. Short sentences. Won't perform neutrality about it.

**Skills granted:** Perception _(Expertise — +7)_, Stealth _(Expertise — +7)_

**Roleplay cue:** Delmar gets louder and less careful. The diplomatic posture goes. He may say things about the Navy he would normally keep to himself, or start clocking a room not for threats but for entry points — windows, servant passages, the door nobody is watching.

## Halythion

Maren knows of [[Halythion-Secrets|Halythion]]. She cannot prove it exists.

What she knows: it is south of the Midchain, near the Tail, in a natural bay guarded by reefs that sailors avoid because ships that go looking do not come back. She has never gone herself — but going where people say not to go is exactly the kind of thing she would do, and she has been thinking about it.

She knows this because she had a friend who claimed to be from there: a sea elf, a strange girl who loved to sing and loved the ocean more than she loved anything else. Maren never pushed for details. The girl offered them anyway, in pieces, over months. Maren does not know if she is still alive.

This is DM-only. Maren's knowledge of Halythion is a hook — she is one of the few people in the world who has a personal lead on the location rather than a tavern rumour.
