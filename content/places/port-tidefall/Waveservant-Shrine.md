# Waveservant Shrine — Port Tidefall

## Overview

The Waveservant Shrine is [[Umberlee|Umberlee]]'s Dockfront shrine in [[Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]], a place where mariners pay before they sail or after they survive.

## At a Glance

| Field | Detail |

| Controlled By | [[Waveservants\|The Waveservants]] |
| Operated By | Junior attendant and senior Waveservant |
| Public Access | Open to mariners and supplicants |
| Known For | Seawater basin, tallow candles, and survival tithes |

## Description

The Waveservant Shrine sits cut into the cliff base at the southern end of the [[Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]] Dockfront. You hear it before you see it — tallow and salt, close and low. Inside: bare stone walls, a seawater basin at the centre, a ring of candles burning. Low ceiling.

## Function

The Waveservants operate on a tithe-and-communion model. Mariners pay before they sail or after they survive. Both are acceptable. Neither is optional. A junior attendant manages the candles and basin; a senior Waveservant manages everything else.

## People & Operations

A junior attendant manages the candles and basin. A senior [[Waveservants|Waveservant]] manages everything else.

## Access & Security

The shrine is open to mariners and supplicants, but the obligation to pay before sailing or after surviving is treated as practical port infrastructure rather than optional devotion.

## Current Hooks

- A mariner who skips tribute creates a social and spiritual problem before the next departure.
