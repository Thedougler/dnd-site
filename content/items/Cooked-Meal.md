# Cooked Meal

[[Rice|Rice]], [[Salt-Pork|pork]], and roast plantain — the evening hot meal at the [[The-Flat-Water|Flat Water]] in [[Kalowe]]. 1 sp. Evenings only; ask about availability. Reweti makes one pot; when it's gone, it's the [[Simple-Meal|simple meal]] or nothing.
