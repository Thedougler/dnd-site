## Overview

**Crisdalynn Khinriss** is a young crow aarakocra monk and cartographer associated with the High Eyrie and the lost _Red Lady_. By the opening of the Shattered Sea campaign, she is fourteen years old, young even among aarakocra whose lives are often shorter than those of humans, and already known in a handful of sailor stories as one of the two survivors of the _Red Lady_ disaster.

Public accounts present her as a contradiction: irreverent and impulsive on deck, exacting and silent in danger. Crewmates remember the jokes, the stolen glittering objects, and the sudden change when a fight begins.

## Appearance

Crisdalynn has blue-black iridescent feathers and the restless build of a crow aarakocra still growing into her own authority. Strangers often see the talons and sharp profile first. Crewmates are more likely to remember the way she pockets anything that catches the light.

## Training

Crisdalynn trained at the [[places/The-High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]], the basalt sea-stack monastery of the Sentinels of the Eyrie. Her training placed her among a people known for watching the [[places/The-Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], keeping records, and treating distance, height, and danger differently than ground-bound sailors.

Her known disciplines include cartography and martial training. She is most often described as unserious until the moment seriousness is required, at which point she becomes precise, fast, and difficult to distract.

## The Red Lady Disaster

Shortly before the campaign begins, Crisdalynn was aboard the _Red Lady_, flagship of Fisk's Fleet, when the fleet was destroyed near the Drowned Maw. As the ship went under, she pulled Admiral [[delmar-summary|Delmar Fisk]] clear from the crow's nest and carried him up through the closing water.

She and Fisk surfaced among wreckage. Two days later, [[lore/Saltwright|the *Saltwright*]] found them and took them aboard. In the stories already spreading around the Midchain, Crisdalynn is the black-feathered scout who flew the admiral out of the Maw when the sea should have taken them both.

## Character

Crisdalynn is irreverent, impulsive, and quick to test boundaries. The public version is easy to caricature: a young crow aarakocra with a satchel of charts, a weakness for shiny objects, and a joke ready at the wrong time.

That impression is incomplete. In combat or crisis, the joking stops. She becomes quiet, technical, and exact. The same instincts that make her hard to govern also make her hard to pin down.

## Known Possessions

- A satchel of charts.
- A crystal dreidel associated with her training.
- A key dangling from one talon, unexplained in public accounts.

## Key Relationships

- **[[delmar-summary|Delmar Fisk]]** — fellow survivor of the _Red Lady_ disaster; the admiral she pulled from the wreck.
- **[[places/The-High-Eyrie|The High Eyrie]]** — her place of training and the institution most associated with her public background.
- **The _Red Lady_** — the lost flagship where she served before the disaster.
- **[[lore/Saltwright|The *Saltwright*]]** — merchant brig that rescued her and Fisk after the wreck.

## Public Standing

Crisdalynn's reputation is still forming. Among those who know only the tale, she is a survivor, scout, and unlikely rescuer from a disaster that should have left no survivors at all. Among those who have shared a deck with her, she is harder to summarize: young, sharp, easily underestimated, and far more dangerous when she stops talking.
