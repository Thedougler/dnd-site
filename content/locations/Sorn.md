# Sorn

## Appearance

A low island by the standards of the [[Verdant-Teeth]], its coastal approach flat and reef-fringed before the land rises into dense canopy. From the water, Sorn shows as a wall of green — elevated limestone ridgeline visible above the treeline on the interior, the jungle running unbroken from the ridge to the shoreline. The coastal fringe is narrow. There is no natural harbour.

The trade beach on the western shore is a strip of pale sand between the reef and the treeline, wide enough for a few boats to pull up at once. The tree line here has been cut back several metres — the only point on the island where the jungle does not press directly to the water. Past it, the canopy closes again. Outsiders do not go past the tree line. Charts for the last thirty years note the interior as restricted. What structures the Botukuri clan have built inland are not visible from the coast.

---

One of the five [[Verdant-Teeth]] islands. Agricultural interior, dense canopy. Home island of the Botukuri clan — birthplace of [[Jean-Claude-Tabarnack]] and [[Simone-Tabarnack]].

Sorn holds the largest slave population in the cluster. Green and blue caste majority handle the labour. A purple-caste garrison maintains order. Since Simone's gold-marking, she commands that garrison.

## Notable Locations

**The Trade Beach** — Western shore, pale sand, treeline cut back a few metres. The only sanctioned point of contact for outsiders. Blue-caste intermediaries come to the waterline; no one comes further inland. Boat traffic only — no permanent docking.

**The Interior** — Not visible from the coast. Agricultural operations, Botukuri clan structures, garrison positions. Outsiders do not enter. Charts annotate the interior as restricted.
