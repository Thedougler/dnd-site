# Places in the Shattered Sea

Ports, islands, storm belts, routes, shrines, ruins, and dangerous water the characters can know about without crossing into GM-only material.

## Region Scale

- [[Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] - warm archipelago of reefs, ports, colonial claims, old ruins, sea tribute, and dangerous routes.
- [[Verdant-Scatter|The Verdant Scatter]] - the main lived-in archipelago between the [[Galewall|Galewall]] and the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]].
- [[Galewall|The Galewall]] - permanent western storm belt separating the Scatter from the colonial homelands.
- [[Central-Strait|The Central Strait]] - main east-west shipping corridor between the [[Crown-Islands|Crown Islands]] and the [[Midchain|Midchain]].
- [[Doldrums|The Doldrums]] - mirror-flat dead water south of the Midchain where sail ships can lose days.
- [[Outer-Reach|The Outer Reach]] - open ocean beyond the Maw, thinly charted and outside ordinary colonial reach.

## Crown Islands

- [[Crown-Islands|The Crown Islands]] - northern arc of large, fortified islands where Dravosi authority is strongest.
- [[Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]] - western gatekeeper island of the Central Strait.
- [[Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]] - Dravosi fortress-port carved into Calder's Tooth.
- [[Fort-Crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] - Dravosi fort crowning Port Tidefall.
- [[Inspection-Pier|Inspection Pier]] - first contact point for official Tidefall arrivals.
- [[Harbourmasters-Office|Harbourmaster's Office]] - administrative office for Tidefall berths, writs, and port paperwork.
- [[Governors-Seat-Tidefall|Governor's Seat]] - upper Tidefall authority and ceremonial centre.
- [[South-Harbour|South Harbour]] - eastern working harbour of Calder's Tooth, less formal than Port Tidefall.
- [[South-Harbour-Impound|South Harbour Impound]] - Crown seizure yard on the east pier.
- [[Oshas-Slip|Osha's Slip]] - mudflat village and lower-profile landing near South Harbour.
- [[High-Line|High-Line]] - overland path and haulage route across Calder's Tooth.
- [[Overland-Track|Overland Track]] - dry route used when the official road is the problem.
- [[Cape-Solitude|Cape Solitude]] - wind-battered northern point of Calder's Tooth.
- [[Calveno|Calveno]] - canal city of witnessed deals, pale stone, and carefully public commerce.
- [[Hook-and-Ledger|The Hook and Ledger]] - Calveno inn and practical meeting point.
- [[Warren|The Warren]] - rumoured Rattkin settlement beneath Calveno.

## The Midchain

- [[Midchain|The Midchain]] - southern arc of close-packed islands, free ports, reefs, and local arrangements.
- [[Kalowe|Kalowe]] - primary Midchain free port and repair harbour.
- [[Aruhe|Aruhe]] - small Midchain island near the Verdant Teeth.
- [[Dath-Mar|Dath-Mar]] - Midchain island known in public route talk.
- [[Dreth|Dreth]] - Verdant Teeth-adjacent island in Grung waters.
- [[Halythion|Halythion]] - sea elf place known more through rumour and fragments than easy visitation.
- [[Meth-Var|Meth-Var]] - Midchain island on public charts.
- [[Orak|Orak]] - eastern support island in Verdant Teeth waters.
- [[Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]] - Midchain island on public charts.

## Verdant Teeth

- [[Verdant-Teeth|The Verdant Teeth]] - Grung-held island cluster south of Kalowe.
- [[Sorn|Sorn]] - reef-fringed Grung island known from the water as green wall, pale sand, and danger.
- [[Karath|Karath]] - Verdant Teeth island with a hostile reputation and no visible invitation to land.
- [[Veth|Veth]] - primary gold-caste island and controlled trade beach.

## The Tail and the Maw

- [[Tail|The Tail]] - eastern convergence of the Scatter near the Drowned Maw.
- [[Drowned-Maw|The Drowned Maw]] - vast eastern trench where charts, compasses, and sailors grow less confident.
- [[High-Eyrie|The High Eyrie]] - basalt sea-stack monastery of the [[Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]].
- [[Shelfworks|The Shelfworks]] - open-water salvage field on the Maw's western shelf.
- [[Shelf-Instrument-Dealer|Shelf Instrument Dealer]] - salvage-market contact point for Antheri instruments.
- [[Antheri-Ruins|Antheri Ruins]] - ancient deep-water structures associated with the Maw and the Shelfworks.

## Beyond the Maw

- [[Sunken-Crown|The Sunken Crown]] - distant eastern island region associated with old Umberlee worship and tabaxi communities.
- [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] - tabaxi settlement island in the Sunken Crown.
- [[Vel-Orn|Vel-Orn]] - eastern chart name sailors may hear before anyone agrees what the name means locally.

## Shops, Shrines, and Useful Stops

- [[Tidefall-Chandlery|Tidefall Chandlery]] - port supply shop for ship goods and practical nautical gear.
- [[Tessarine-Trade-House-Tidefall|Tessarine Trade House, Tidefall]] - Concordat office in Dravosi territory.
- [[Umberlee-Shrine-Sunken-Crown|Umberlee Shrine, Sunken Crown]] - old shrine associated with eastern worship.
