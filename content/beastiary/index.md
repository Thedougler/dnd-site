# Bestiary

Every sailor working the Scatter has a story. The sea that fills the space between islands is not empty — something is always in the water, in the canopy, or waiting in the dark of a sealed ruin. Some of what follows is confirmed. Some of it is rumour. The distinction matters less than you might hope.

## On the Water

- [[beastiary/sea-life/index|Sea Life]] — Ocean predators, sharks, krakens, and the things sighted from the rail at night
- [[beastiary/dragons/index|Dragons]] — Dragon turtles, sea serpents, and true dragons; encountering one is a survival problem, not a fight

## On Land

- [[beastiary/beasts/index|Beasts]] — Natural predators, wildlife, and swarms across the Scatter's islands
- [[beastiary/fey/index|Fey]] — Goblin warbands found in the interior forests and hill country
- [[beastiary/monstrosity/index|Monstrosities]] — Harpies, rocs, and creatures that don't fit a cleaner category

## In the Deep Places

- [[beastiary/fiend/index|Fiends]] — Sahuagin who raid from the shelf walls and outer reef drop-offs
- [[beastiary/plants/index|Plants]] — Myconid colonies and fungal hazards of the underdark beneath the Scatter
- [[beastiary/ooze/index|Oozes]] — Dissolving predators of the deep underdark; no confirmed surface sightings

## Made Things and Outliers

- [[beastiary/humanoid/index|Humanoids]] — Pirates, admirals, and the people most likely to try to take your ship
- [[beastiary/constructs/index|Constructs]] — Animated objects and magical sentinels left behind in sealed halls and old collections
- [[beastiary/celestial/index|Celestials]] — Sacred creatures; encountering one is rarely accidental
- [[beastiary/elementals/index|Elementals]] — Elemental-bound creatures, including what the Ashwall pilots believe keeps the [[Galewall|Galewall]] burning
