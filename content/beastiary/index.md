# Bestiary

The [[Shattered-Sea|Shattered Sea]] has many dangerous things that call it home. Some entries are confirmed creatures, others are simply rumours.

## Sea Monsters and Ocean Threats

- [[beastiary/Leviathan|The Leviathan]] - A rumoured titanic black eel that boils the sea around it, sightings limited to within the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]
- [[Sawek|Sawek (Blue Devil)]] - Ambush predator known for lurking in blue holes in the [[Central-Strait]], [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] and [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]
- [[Whip-Shark|Whip Shark (Welak)]] - Open-ocean hunter, big enough to sink small ships, found in [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], particularly the [[Shelfworks]], and most open deep ocean like [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] or along the [[Galewall|Galewall]]
- [[ancient-dragon-turtle|Ancient Dragon Turtle]] - Mythic deep-water predator known for boiling seas and scalding steam breath, found in the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], the open water beyond, and around the [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] Blue Hole
- [[beastiary/kraken|Kraken]] - Titanic deep-sea predator capable of destroying ships and calling lightning, rumoured to lair in the abyssal depths of the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] and beyond the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]
- [[beastiary/dragon-turtle|Dragon Turtle]] - Large enough to break a working boat, known to lurk at the edge of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]] Blue Hole and the eastern approaches of the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]]
- [[ancient-sea-serpent|Ancient Sea Serpent]] - Cold-current predator that moves against the wind, haunting the deep lanes of the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] and the shelf-edge waters where [[places/tail/index|the Tail]] sends its longline crews
- [[young-dragon-turtle|Young Dragon Turtle]] - Dangerous enough to capsize small vessels, found where reef shallows give way to deep water around the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]] and the eastern [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]]
- [[beastiary/young-sea-serpent|Young Sea Serpent]] - Follows warm currents through the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] and along [[places/tail/index|the Tail's]] shelf edge; big enough to constrict and sink a small vessel
- [[beastiary/giant-squid|Giant Squid]] - Deep-water ambush predator that rises from the drop-off at night; a known hazard for divers at the [[Shelfworks|Shelfworks]] and a practical explanation for unexplained disappearances in the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]
- [[beastiary/giant-shark|Giant Shark]] - Follows cold upwellings and blood trails; a serious threat near the [[Galewall|Galewall's]] wreckage and along [[places/tail/index|the Tail's]] deep longline grounds
- [[dragon-turtle-wyrmling|Dragon Turtle Wyrmling]] - Still large enough to wreck a skiff and boil a reef channel; sightings cluster around the outer beaches and reef approaches of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]], particularly near [[Meth-Var|Meth-Var]]
- [[beastiary/killer-whale|Killer Whale]] - Hunts in pods through the cold open water of the [[Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] and the storm-dark approaches to the [[Galewall|Galewall]]; known to follow lifeboats for hours
- [[beastiary/hunter-shark|Hunter Shark]] - The common working shark of the Scatter, following ship routes and harbour chum through the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]], [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] approaches, and the reef gap at [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]
- [[beastiary/giant-octopus|Giant Octopus]] - Reef-dwelling ambush predator found in the rocky shallows of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] and around the reef formations of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-piranhas|Swarm of Piranhas]] - Found in the still interior lakes and spring-fed pools of [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] islands; crews watering at places like Vatumei are advised to lower casks on a line rather than wade in
- [[beastiary/giant-seahorse|Giant Seahorse]] - Large enough to serve as a mount; found in the sheltered reef waters of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] and the [[Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]]
- [[beastiary/reef-shark|Reef Shark]] - Common in the shallow reef channels of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] and the turquoise shallows of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]; a routine hazard for swimmers and divers

## Raiders and Coastal Foes

- [[beastiary/pirate-admiral|Pirate Admiral]] - A fleet commander who controls a stretch of sea rather than just a ship; the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] trade lanes, the [[places/tail/index|Tail's]] approach to the Drowned Maw, and the Ashwall lee of the [[Galewall|Galewall]] are the places where captains of this calibre extract tolls
- [[beastiary/pirate-captain|Pirate Captain]] - Dangerous enough to take a working vessel; operates across the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]], the [[Shelfworks|Shelfworks]] salvage lanes, the approaches to the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], and the reef gap at [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]
- [[beastiary/sahuagin-baron|Sahuagin Baron]] - Warlord of a sahuagin warband; commands shark-riders from the deep shelf of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] and the outer reef walls of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]] where the bottom drops away fast
- [[beastiary/sahuagin-priest|Sahuagin Priest]] - Conducts blood rites at submerged shrines in the deep-water blue holes of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]; encountered where sahuagin warbands gather before a raid
- [[beastiary/pirate|Pirate]] - The common crew of every raiding vessel in the Scatter; found everywhere ships go, from the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] and [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] anchorages to the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] approaches and the reef gap at [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]
- [[beastiary/sahuagin-warrior|Sahuagin Warrior]] - The raiding soldiers of the deep; encountered in packs along the shelf walls and reef drop-offs of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], often accompanying a Baron or Priest

## Legendary, Magical, and Supernatural

- [[arclight-phoenix|Arclight Phoenix]] - Born in the volcanic vents of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]] and believed by Ashwall pilots to be what sustains the [[Galewall|Galewall]] itself; seen as lightning-bright light moving inside the storm, bright enough to draw lines across closed eyelids
- [[beastiary/roc|Roc]] - Nests on the high basalt sea stacks near the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]] and the northeastern Crown cliffs; large enough to carry off a longboat, and the Sentinels do not consider them tame neighbours
- [[beastiary/guardian-naga|Guardian Naga]] - Ancient guardian of a jungle shrine somewhere in the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain's]] interior; the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] islands have ruins older than any colonial map, and the locals know which temples are not visited twice
- [[beastiary/giant-ape|Giant Ape]] - Territorial apex predator of the dense rainforest interiors of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] and the larger [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] islands; sightings at [[Sorn|Sorn]] suggest at least one family group controls the highland canopy there
- [[beastiary/giant-axe-beak|Giant Axe Beak]] - Aggressive flightless predator found on the clifftop scrublands of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]], the exposed ridgelines of [[Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]], and the coastal heath of [[places/tail/index|the Tail]]; fast enough to run down a horse
- [[beastiary/unicorn|Unicorn]] - Unconfirmed; a single account from a [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] interior expedition describes a white-maned creature standing at the edge of a jungle clearing that vanished before anyone else could see it — no physical evidence, no second witness, filed under rumour
- [[beastiary/owlbear|Owlbear]] - Ambush predator of the dense interior forests of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; moves silently for its size and attacks without warning — the screech is usually the last thing heard before contact
- [[beastiary/pegasus|Pegasus]] - Rumoured to range the updrafts above the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]]; the Sentinels claim sightings but treat the subject as a private matter — no confirmed encounter, no physical evidence
- [[beastiary/sea-hag|Sea Hag]] - Fey predator of isolated coastal caves and the wreck-strewn shallows of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]; approaches ships in the guise of a survivor or a drowning woman, and the ugliness underneath kills before the claws do
- [[beastiary/giant-eagle|Giant Eagle]] - Nests in the clifftop aeries of the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]]; large enough to carry a grown sailor and intelligent enough to be offended by the attempt — the Sentinels maintain an uneasy accord with them
- [[beastiary/giant-vulture|Giant Vulture]] - Carrion bird found wherever the sea deposits its dead; circles the clifftops of the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]], the black rock beaches of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]], the wreck-heavy coastline of [[places/tail/index|the Tail]], and the exposed shoals around [[Meth-Var|Meth-Var]]
- [[beastiary/harpy|Harpy]] - Nests in the sea cliffs of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]], [[Meth-Var|Meth-Var]], and the rockier stretches of [[places/tail/index|the Tail]]; the song carries further than it should and sailors who follow it don't come back
- [[beastiary/giant-owl|Giant Owl]] - Nocturnal forest hunter of the old-growth interiors of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; near-silent on the wing and regarded by island hunters as a sign the old forest is intact — if the owls are gone, the canopy has been cleared

## Constructs and Animated Objects

- [[beastiary/animated-rug-of-smothering|Animated Rug of Smothering]] - A ward left among the wares at [[Cats-Curios|Cat's Curios]]; the standing advice in the Midchain is to buy something before you linger too long over the merchandise, and to keep your hands where they can be seen
- [[beastiary/animated-armor|Animated Armor]] - Remnant sentinel of a sealed hall or locked collection in [[Calveno|Calveno]]; typically motionless until a threshold is crossed — explorers in the older quarter learn to knock before entering rooms that look undisturbed
- [[beastiary/animated-broom|Animated Broom]] - Domestic construct in active service at [[Cats-Curios|Cat's Curios]]; considerably faster than it looks and has been known to sweep unannounced visitors out the door before the owner needs to speak
- [[beastiary/animated-flying-sword|Animated Flying Sword]] - Freed guard-blade from a triggered ward or sprung mechanism; encountered in sealed vaults and abandoned halls in [[Calveno|Calveno]] — it does not distinguish between intruder and rescuer

## Beasts and Swarms

- [[beastiary/giant-crocodile|Giant Crocodile]] - Apex ambush predator of the tidal rivers and mangrove channels of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]]; large enough to take a longboat off a bank, and most crews don't see it until the boat is already moving
- [[giant-scorpion|Giant Scorpion]] - Found in the dry rocky terrain of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]] and the drier interior elevations of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]]; hides under flat stone and inside hollow logs — a hazard for any party making camp ashore
- [[beastiary/giant-boar|Giant Boar]] - Charges without warning from the undergrowth of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]], the [[Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]], and [[Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]]; big enough to stave in a ship's boat, and parties heading inland for water should post a watch before following jungle trails
- [[beastiary/giant-constrictor-snake|Giant Constrictor Snake]] - Found in the rainforest interiors of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] and the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]], including confirmed sightings on Dreth, Orak, and Osha's Slip; large enough to pin and suffocate a horse, and often found near watering holes
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-venomous-snakes|Swarm of Venomous Snakes]] - Disturbed nest or flooded burrow on [[Orak|Orak]]; the interior undergrowth harbours more than it shows, and a machete through the leaf litter will flush one before you step in it
- [[beastiary/giant-spider|Giant Spider]] - Web-builder of the deep jungle interior; found across the [[Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]], [[Karath|Karath]], the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]], [[Orak|Orak]], Veth, and Aruhe — the webs are fine enough to miss until you're already in them
- [[beastiary/giant-toad|Giant Toad]] - Ambush predator of the boggy lowlands and freshwater margins of [[Karath|Karath]]; sits motionless in shallow water or thick mud and strikes faster than its bulk suggests
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-insects|Swarm of Insects]] - The working hazard of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] jungle floor; encountered wherever standing water or rotting wood concentrates them — biting clouds that can force a camp to move in under an hour
- [[beastiary/giant-wasp|Giant Wasp]] - Nests in cliff overhangs and hollow shoreline trees around [[The-Doldrums|the Doldrums]], [[Kalowe|Kalowe]], [[Sorn|Sorn]], and [[Dreth|Dreth]]; disturbing one nest sends the whole colony
- [[beastiary/boar|Boar]] - Common feral hog of the scrubland and coastal heath of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; hunted for meat but approached with caution — sows with young are as dangerous as the tuskers
- [[beastiary/constrictor-snake|Constrictor Snake]] - Common tree-and-water snake of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] lowlands; large enough to take a dog, and parties sheltering under riverside vegetation should check the branches before making camp
- [[beastiary/giant-bat|Giant Bat]] - Cave-roosting hunter of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]], [[places/tail/index|the Tail]], the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]], and [[Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]]; emerges at dusk in columns thick enough to blot the stars — the colonies in some Sunken Crown sea caves have been there longer than any settlement nearby
- [[beastiary/giant-centipede|Giant Centipede]] - Found under loose stone and damp timber throughout [[Sorn|Sorn]]; bites rather than charges, and the venom is enough to put a man off his feet for a day
- [[beastiary/giant-frog|Giant Frog]] - Marshland and tidal-flat ambush predator of [[Sorn|Sorn]], the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]], [[Orak|Orak]], [[Dreth|Dreth]], Veth, and Aruhe; waits in shallow water with only its eyes above the surface — a common hazard for parties crossing low ground at night
- [[beastiary/giant-lizard|Giant Lizard]] - Rocky-shore and ruin predator found in the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]], [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]], [[Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]], and Dath-Mar; territorial and fast — disturbing a basking lizard in a ruin entrance is a reliable way to start a fight before the real exploration begins
- [[beastiary/giant-venomous-snake|Giant Venomous Snake]] - Large ambush serpent of the mid-canopy and river margins in the [[Verdant-Scatter|Verdant Scatter]], [[Karath|Karath]], the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]], Aruhe, and Veth; territorial near water and strikes at leg height without warning
- [[beastiary/giant-wolf-spider|Giant Wolf Spider]] - Hunting spider of the undergrowth and dry forest litter of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]], [[Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]], [[Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]], and [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]]; doesn't web, runs fast, and is commonly found in dry-season camps after dark
- [[beastiary/panther|Panther]] - Silent apex predator of the dense canopy forest of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]]; a party may be stalked for hours before it commits — the one that's seen is rarely the only one
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-bats|Swarm of Bats]] - Vast cave colony of the sea-cliff overhangs of the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]]; the evening departure sounds like distant thunder and lasts long enough for sailors to stop what they're doing and watch
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-rats|Swarm of Rats]] - Hull-rot hazard of [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]'s waterfront; infests badly-kept stowage and spreads between vessels in harbour — a ship's cat earns its keep in port
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-ravens|Swarm of Ravens]] - Carrion flock from the cliffs of [[Vel-Orn|Vel-Orn]] and the exposed rock of [[Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]]; follows ships in distress and strips a body in minutes — their circling over a vessel is a universally understood bad sign
- [[beastiary/giant-crab|Giant Crab]] - Intertidal heavyweight of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] beaches; large enough to shear rope and patient enough to wait in a tidal pool for hours until something edible arrives
- [[beastiary/giant-rat|Giant Rat]] - Dock-bred pest of [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]'s warehouses and waterfront; larger than a cat and aggressive when cornered — found wherever cargo has been sitting too long
- [[beastiary/giant-weasel|Giant Weasel]] - Burrow hunter of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] forest floor; fast, aggressive for its size, and disinclined to retreat — occasionally found in cargo holds after a Crown Islands layover
- [[beastiary/venomous-snake|Venomous Snake]] - Common bite hazard of the [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] undergrowth; small enough to go unnoticed until stepped on, and the venom is worth treating seriously
- [[public/beastiary/bat|Bat]] - Common cave roost species throughout the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]]; not a threat alone but the guano in old roost caves makes some ruins hazardous underfoot and the air unpleasant
- [[public/beastiary/cat|Cat]] - Common dockside and alley presence in [[Calveno|Calveno]]; kept as working ratcatchers aboard ships as well as in warehouses — a useful working animal that earns its passage in port
- [[public/beastiary/crab|Crab]] - Tidal flat species throughout the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]]; edible, abundant, and a reliable indicator of shallow reef systems — if the crabs have cleared out of an area, something larger arrived first
- [[public/beastiary/eagle|Eagle]] - Thermal-riding raptor of the [[High-Eyrie|High Eyrie]] cliffs; a useful navigation marker from the water — a circling eagle reliably indicates a rock formation or exposed shelf below
- [[public/beastiary/frog|Frog]] - Abundant wetland species in [[Orak|Orak]]'s river margins and interior swamp; their sudden silence at dusk is a reliable warning that something larger has entered the area
- [[public/beastiary/giant-fire-beetle|Giant Fire Beetle]] - Bioluminescent cave beetle found in [[The-Warren|The Warren]]; the gland oil holds its light for days after extraction and is harvested as a cheap lamp substitute — an unusual trade good from the depths of the Ashwall
- [[public/beastiary/goat|Goat]] - Feral herd animal of the high rocky terrain around [[High-Line|High-Line]]; edible and useful to parties resupplying ashore, and their presence on a cliff face is a reliable guide to traversable routes
- [[public/beastiary/lizard|Lizard]] - Small rocky-shore species found throughout the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]; harmless and fast — their alarm sprint is often the first indication that something larger is moving through the rocks ahead
- [[public/beastiary/owl|Owl]] - Common night hunter of the [[places/crown-islands/index|Crown Islands]] woodland; heard more reliably than seen — the sudden silence of owls after dark is an early warning that something has disturbed them
- [[public/beastiary/rat|Rat]] - Ubiquitous dock pest found in every harbour in the Scatter, concentrated in [[Kalowe|Kalowe]]'s waterfront storehouses; a standard hazard of stored cargo and the chief reason a ship's cat earns its keep
- [[public/beastiary/raven|Raven]] - Cliff nester of [[Vel-Orn|Vel-Orn]] in the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]; intelligent enough to remember faces and observed in trading ports picking through the leavings of ships that have been in harbour before
- [[public/beastiary/scorpion|Scorpion]] - Ambush predator under loose rock and dry debris throughout the [[Ashwall-Islands|Ashwall Islands]]; the sting is serious despite the size — shake out every boot, bedroll, and pack before dawn

## Fiendish Swarms

- [[swarm-of-lemures|Swarm of Lemures]] - Recorded near the approaches to the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]; masses of half-formed damned that flow toward heat and sound — no one who has gone deep into the Maw's channels denies what they saw
- [[swarm-of-larvae|Swarm of Larvae]] - Linked to planar scarring near [[Antheri-Ruins|Antheri Ruins]]; larval forms of the damned that swarm toward the living — encountered by those who dig too far into the ruins' lower levels
