# Wound Dressing

Salt-treated linen bandages and wound cloths, sold in lots of 10. 5 sp. Used to dress and bind wounds after initial treatment. Not a substitute for a [[Healers-Kit|healer's kit]] for stabilisation, but extends what the kit can do.
