# Karath

## Overview

Karath looks like the other [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] from the water — low reef approaches, rainforest running close to the shore, no harbour, no smoke, no visible reason to stop. The distinction is what happens when you get close anyway. Outsiders who nose into the reef gaps are met with dart fire, no warning, no gap between the quiet beach and the response. Pilots who know these waters treat Karath specifically as the island you give extra room, not because the others are inviting but because Karath is the one with a reputation for not waiting to see what you were planning.

[[places/midchain/index|The Midchain]] coastal settlements have noticed the raids out of this direction have been getting worse. [[Chain-Council|The Chain Council]] has received the complaints. Whether either of those facts will produce consequences is an open question the people living near the water are watching closely.

Karath's reputation belongs to the grung first, but the island helps maintain it. The forest behind the dart line is known for [[beastiary/beasts/giant-spider|giant spiders]], [[beastiary/beasts/giant-venomous-snake|giant venomous snakes]], and [[beastiary/beasts/giant-toad|giant toads]] in the wet cuts between ridges. Outsiders who imagine the beach is the dangerous part have misunderstood the warning.

---

## Fauna

The interior forest maintains the island's reputation past the waterline. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-spider|Giant spiders]] occupy the older canopy growth and the shadow between ridge lines — the web structures are large enough to be visible from the forest floor and functional enough to catch things considerably larger than insects. The grung paths move around them rather than through them.

[[beastiary/beasts/giant-venomous-snake|Giant venomous snakes]] work the root tangles and fallen timber along the wet ridge cuts. They are a category of hazard that does not announce itself, and in a forest where outsiders are already under dart fire from above, a snake strike from ground level arrives without warning in either direction. Grung hunters move through this forest; anyone else is navigating terrain that has never been cleared for them.

The wet cuts between ridges pool standing water year-round, which suits [[beastiary/beasts/giant-toad|giant toads]] well. They are ambush feeders with the patience the environment rewards, and large enough to take prey that underestimates the reach of something that has been sitting motionless in the same pool since before the current season. Outsiders who imagine the beach is the dangerous part of Karath have not thought through the rest of the island.
