# Cat's Curios

A narrow door in a [[places/calveno/index|Calveno]] side-street — brass fittings, no name. Inside: long benches of half-finished mechanisms, a low forge, the smell of hot oil and soldering flux, and a locked glass cabinet along one wall holding pieces that don't belong to this century.

The workshop belongs to [[Catarina-DaVirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]. She does not advertise. She does not take walk-ins. Finding her requires a referral from someone she trusts, and she does not expand that circle casually.

---

## Notable Locations

- **Workshop Floor** — Long benches, precision tools racked by size, half-finished mechanisms in various states of assembly. All transactions happen here. She works from the bench and does not offer chairs.

- **Specimen Cabinet** — A locked glass case along the east wall. Dark-alloy pieces, faintly iridescent, tooling marks too fine for any current forge in the Scatter. Trade only; no cash price offered.
