# Session 01 Recap — The Saltwright

_Canon as delivered. Last updated 2026-05-14._

---

The hold of the [[Saltwright]] was dark and close, smelling of bilge water and old rope, and the party waited in it like a held breath. Above, they could hear [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont]]'s voice carrying across the deck, easy and unhurried, keeping [[Barnaby-Rook|Barnaby Rook]]'s attention fixed at the wheel while three of his crew descended the ladder into the dark below.

The Enforcer came first. The crew had taken to calling him Cap'n Gorgeous, and the name wasn't wrong. Tall, broadly built, golden hair that caught even the thin light of the hold. He walked in without hesitation. A [[Musket|musket]] shot answered him. The crack of it in that enclosed space was enormous, and for a ringing, disorienting moment both [[Delmar-Fisk|Delmar]] and [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss|Crisdalynn]] lost the world to white noise. The alarm went up. But [[Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin]] had laid a Minor Illusion across the doorway they were sheltering behind, and Crisdalynn worked fast, dragging Cap'n Gorgeous back through it before the sound could be placed. The two deckhands came down cautious, uncertain, not knowing exactly what they were walking toward. They found out. Neither of them made it back up.

[[Stripes-Bitemore|Stripes]] was not present this session.

Delmar knelt at the foot of the ladder and Crisdalynn didn't slow down. She hit his hands at a run and he launched her up through the hatch like a crack of thunder. Her wings snapped open the moment she cleared the weather deck and sent the two nearest Dravosi sprawling. Perrin and [[Jean-Claude-Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] emerged through a gap in the decking at a second angle, catching the remaining crew between them. It was over quickly. Both Dravosi Enforcers died in the press of it. When the last deckhand, a young man maybe nineteen with frightened eyes, dropped his sword and went still, Rook's voice cut across the deck from the gangplank.

_"I'll shoot you myself for that, turncoat."_

Delmar looked at the boy. "Would you rather fight for men who'll threaten to shoot you, or men who'll protect you?"

[[Geoffrey-Draves|Geoffrey Draves]] thought about it for about as long as it took him to answer. _"I'll do whatever keeps me alive today."_

He picked the sword back up and joined the fight. He is, by the current count, the party's entire crew.

The gangplank between the [[Saltwright]] and the [[HCS-Surety|HCS Surety]] was where things got complicated. Jean-Claude opened with the [[Decanter-of-Endless-Water|Decanter of Endless Water]] and the blast caught Rook full in the chest, throwing him off the plank and buying the party precious seconds to deal with the Enforcer and the Alchemist before he could rejoin. The Enforcer fell. The Alchemist took enough punishment to make her desperate, and desperation made her reach for the canister on her bandolier. The gas rolled out across the deck and caught Jean-Claude and Perrin both. They resisted it. [[Crissdalynn-Khinriss|Crisdalynn]] answered with Gust and sent the cloud rolling back toward the Alchemist.

Jean-Claude placed it in the same moment the fumes reached him. The preparation method was [[Simone-Tabarnack|Simone]]'s. Specifically hers. His sister is supplying the [[Dravosi-Crown|Dravosi Crown]] with [[Grung-Clans|Grung]]-made toxin, and the proof of it was drifting across the decking between two ships in the Shattered Sea. He said nothing. The fight continued.

The Alchemist was brought down. Rook hauled himself back over the rail, raised his flintlock at [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont]], and fired. The round struck the ridged plate of Beaumont's [[Salvaged-Antheri-Plate|shell]] at his shoulder and spun away into the water.

Beaumont picked up a boat hook.

_"Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'."_

That's where we left it.
