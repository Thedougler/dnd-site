# Handaxe

A short iron-headed axe, single-bitted, with a wooden haft. Works equally well for splitting kindling and boarding actions. Throwable. 5 gp.

**Properties \[RAW]:** _Simple melee weapon._ 1d6 slashing. Light, Thrown (range 20/60).
