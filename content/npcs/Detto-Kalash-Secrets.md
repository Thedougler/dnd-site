# [[Detto-Kalash|Detto Kalash]]

_Stub — created from fleet records. No dedicated source yet._

Captain of [[Heft-Secrets]], [[Fisks-Fleet-Secrets]]'s heavy ship. Earth Genasi. Used Mold Earth to open the passage from raw/ingested/Umberlee's shrine island's submerged base directly into the shrine during the Pearl theft.

Drowned when Umberlee sank the fleet over the [[Drowned-Maw-Secrets|Drowned Maw]]. Soul pressed into [[private/players/Delmar-Fisk]] by Umberlee at the moment of sinking.

---

## Mortis Voice

**Roleplay Prompt:** Drax as a pirate captain, who says the true thing, immediately, every time.

**Personality in Fisk's head:** Blunt, physical, enduring. No charm, no angle — just what is true and what needs doing. When Detto surfaces, Delmar stops managing the room and starts stating facts. Quieter, not louder — the kind of stillness that takes up more space than noise. Doesn't dislike people; simply has no interest in performing for them.

**Forced trigger:** Delmar fails a Constitution saving throw, or is knocked prone or grappled. Detto surfaces when the body becomes the problem.

**Skills granted:** Athletics _(Expertise — +5)_, Survival

**Roleplay cue:** Delmar gets quiet and direct. Drops the vocabulary. "No" is a complete sentence. If he is hurt, he doesn't hide it — he moves through it and expects everyone else to do the same.
