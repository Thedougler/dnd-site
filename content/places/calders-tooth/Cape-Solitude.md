# Cape Solitude

## Overview

Cape Solitude is the northernmost point of [[Calders-Tooth-Overview|Calder's Tooth]], where the island runs out of island and faces open ocean directly. Heavy surf, constant wind off the [[Galewall|Galewall]], and no shelter. Local fisherfolk won't anchor within two miles of the cape. They don't explain why if you ask them directly, but the short version is the Dead Light.

## Description

Cape Solitude faces open ocean with no shelter: hard surf, constant Galewall wind, boulder fields at the cape base, updrafts along the cliff, and a ruined pre-colonial tower standing on the point.

## Function

The cape is a navigational warning, a local taboo, and a potential investigation site. The ruined tower and vanished crews make it useful as a mystery location rather than a routine stop.

## Access & Approach

The approach is exposed to open ocean and heavy surf. Local fisherfolk avoid anchoring within two miles. The cape base is a boulder field with aggressive giant crabs, and the clifftop wind makes voices carry in ways locals do not trust.

## People & Power

The [[Dravosi-Crown|Dravosi Crown]] attempted a lighthouse project here and abandoned it after three successive work crews disappeared without signs of struggle. No bodies, no tools left behind, nothing disturbed. The project was quietly struck from the ledgers.

## Notable Features

**The Dead Light** — A ruined stone tower on the point, pre-colonial construction older than the Crown's claim to the island.

**Abandoned Lighthouse Project** — Three Crown work crews vanished here without signs of struggle, leaving the tower untouched and the ledgers quietly edited.

## Flora

No specific flora is documented. The cape is defined by exposed cliff, open wind, heavy surf, and boulder fields.

## Fauna

The surf at the cape base runs hard against a boulder field, which suits [[beastiary/beasts/giant-crab|giant crabs]] — the colony here is larger than the ones on Calder's Tooth's eastern mudflats, and more aggressive. The constant swell keeps food washing in and keeps casual visitors away from the waterline, which the crabs appear to rely on. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-vulture|Giant vultures]] use the cape's updrafts daily, circling the point in numbers that suggest they expect something to die here regularly. Given the cape's history, this is not a baseless expectation.

The locals' reluctance to anchor near the cape predates the lighthouse project. The fisherfolk say voices carry from the clifftop on windy nights — not wind noise, but voices with phrasing in them. [[beastiary/monstrosity/harpy|Harpies]] nest in cliff-face crevices across the Calder's Tooth northern headland and are known to be present on this stretch of coast. Whether the three lighthouse crews followed a sound toward the cliff edge is a question nobody has gone back to confirm.

## Hazards

Cape Solitude's hazards are open-ocean exposure, heavy surf, giant crabs, harpy cliff voices, giant vultures, and the unresolved disappearance of three lighthouse crews.

## Current Hooks

- The tower still stands. Nothing has moved into it that anyone has reported, and no one has gone back to look carefully.
- Whether the vanished lighthouse crews followed a sound toward the cliff edge remains unconfirmed.
