# Perrin Black-Jaw

> _Built a ship to fish and support his family. Lost it to something vast coming up from below. Now hunting it._

## Overview

**Perrin Black-Jaw**, sometimes shortened to **PBJ** by those who know him, is a Rattkin sailor, negotiator, and survivor of the destruction of the _Vestra_. By the opening of the Shattered Sea campaign, he is ten years old, middle-aged by Rattkin reckoning, and travelling under the weight of a shipwreck his family does not yet fully understand.

Perrin is not built for heavy ship work by size or constitution. He made himself useful another way: reading people, building contacts, finding leverage, and turning small advantages into durable arrangements. In the language of docks and holds, he is both diplomat and operator.

## Appearance

Perrin is a well-dressed fancy rat Rattkin, commonly seen in an olive-drab cloak. He carries himself like someone who has already counted the exits and priced the room. His small size makes him easy to underestimate in the open, but less so in the spaces Rattkin know best: gaps, holds, alleys, bilges, and crowded docks.

## Background

Perrin comes from [[The-Warren|the Tangle]], the Rattkin family network at the heart of [[The-Warren]]. He was raised around the practical lessons of the Run: read people, stay useful, build capital, and understand how the Dry can kill those who move through it carelessly.

Over time, Perrin turned contacts into capital, capital into crew, and crew into a working ship. That ship, the _Vestra_, had belonged to his grandfather and passed to Perrin after his grandfather's death. It was therefore both livelihood and inheritance: a practical fishing vessel, a family asset, and the last major thing his grandfather left behind.

## The Loss of the Vestra

The _Vestra_ was destroyed by something that rose from the deep near the Drowned Maw. Perrin survived, but the ship was lost. Public accounts describe the attacker only in broad terms: vast, otherworldly, and coming from below. Perrin does not yet have a full explanation, but he has made the pursuit of one central to his life.

The loss is not merely financial. Perrin's family does not yet know the full extent of what happened to the _Vestra_. He is trying to solve the matter before he has to report the failure home, a delay that has become its own burden.

After the wreck, Perrin was shipwrecked on [[places/Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] and later stowed away aboard [[lore/Saltwright|the *Saltwright*]]. At the campaign's opening, he is hidden aboard the merchant brig while its other passengers and crises begin to converge.

## Patronage

Since the sinking, Perrin has been contacted by a large and otherworldly presence connected to the events around the _Vestra_. He was not a religious man before the wreck, and his present relationship to belief remains unsettled. What is clear is that the ship's destruction changed more than his finances; it placed him in contact with powers and questions larger than ordinary fishing work.

## Character

Perrin is charming, practical, and ethically selective in the manner of someone raised to survive among people with more reach than mercy. Manipulation, bribery, and leverage are tools he understands. Some lines remain firm: he does not traffic people, and he does not mistreat those who cannot fight back.

His stated aims are layered. He wants revenge on the thing that destroyed the _Vestra_. He wants to fish again. He wants to return home with enough standing to become an elder of the Tangle, teaching young Rattkin how to move through the Dry without getting killed.

- **[[lore/Saltwright|The *Saltwright*]]** — merchant brig where Perrin hides after the destruction of the _Vestra_.
- **[[crissdalynn-summary|Crisdalynn Khinriss]]** — fellow party member whose avian nature complicates Perrin's instincts under stress.

## Public Standing

Perrin's public story is still mostly unwritten outside Rattkin circles: a small, well-dressed survivor of a destroyed ship, carrying a family heirloom and hunting the thing that came from below. To those who know the Tangle, the loss of the _Vestra_ is not only a shipwreck. It is a family matter, a political embarrassment, and an inheritance at the bottom of the sea.
