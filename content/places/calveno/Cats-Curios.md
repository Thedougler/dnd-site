# Cat's Curios

![[raw/assets/banners/Catarina-DaVirelli.webp|Catarina Da'Virelli, proprietor of Cat's Curios]]

## Overview

Cat's Curios is a referral-only artificer workshop in [[Calveno]], hidden behind a narrow side-street door with brass fittings and no name. It belongs to [[Catarina-DaVirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]], who does not advertise, does not take walk-ins, and does not expand her circle casually.

## Description

A narrow door in a [[Calveno]] side-street — brass fittings, no name. Inside: long benches of half-finished mechanisms, a low forge, the smell of hot oil and soldering flux, and a locked glass cabinet along one wall holding pieces that don't belong to this century.

## Function

Cat's Curios is where Catarina handles precision artificer work, commissions, repairs, referrals, and trade involving unusual mechanisms. Finding her requires a referral from someone she trusts.

## People & Operations

The workshop belongs to [[Catarina-DaVirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]]. She works from the bench and does not offer chairs. All transactions happen on the workshop floor, and the circle of people who can reach her is deliberately small.

## Notable Areas

**Workshop Floor** — Long benches, precision tools racked by size, half-finished mechanisms in various states of assembly. All transactions happen here. She works from the bench and does not offer chairs.

**Specimen Cabinet** — A locked glass case along the east wall. Dark-alloy pieces, faintly iridescent, tooling marks too fine for any current forge in the Scatter. Trade only; no cash price offered.

## Stock / Services

- ask — [[Artificer-Commission|Artificer commission]] — _"Describe the mechanism. I'll tell you if it's something I'll do, and what it takes."_
- ask — [[Mechanism-Repair|Mechanism repair]] — _"Bring it in. I'll assess it on the bench."_
- trade — [[Antheri-Mechanism|Antheri-alloy mechanisms (specimen cabinet)]] — _"Trade only. No cash price. What you're offering matters more than what you're offering to pay."_

## Access & Security

There is no sign and no public shopfront. Access depends on referral, reputation, and Catarina's willingness to accept the visitor. The specimen cabinet is locked, and the most important pieces are trade-only rather than cash-priced.

## Current Hooks

- A party that needs the Scatter's best artificer work needs a referral from someone Catarina trusts.
- The dark-alloy pieces in the specimen cabinet connect the shop to the Antheri salvage economy.
