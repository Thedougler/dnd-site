# The Saltwright

> [!READ-ALOUD]
> A two-masted merchant brig rides broad and low in the water, her hull worked pale grey-brown by years of salt and weather. Nothing about her is ornamental: no figurehead, no bright rail, no paint left where the sea could take it. Below, the smell is brine, old cedar, and the cold deep water pressing through the seams. Every timber sounds used.

## Current State

The *Saltwright* is underway on her regular [[The-Midchain]] cargo run under [[Beaumont-Sel]]. In [[Session-01]] she carries salt, dried fish, and cloth out of [[Kalowe]], riding low but steady as an ordinary working cargo brig.

## Overview

A two-masted merchant brig out of [[Kalowe]], built for trade rather than display. She is old, plain, and thoroughly practical: a deep hold, a small captain's cabin, and no space wasted on comfort or prestige.

## History

[[Beaumont-Sel]] has captained the ship on this route for eleven years. Shortly before the campaign opened, [[Perrin-Black-Jaw]] stowed away aboard the Saltwright at [[Keth-Naar]] after the Leviathan destroyed [[Vestra]]. [[Belmore]] bartered for passage west from the same harbour after his tribe sent him to learn why Umberlee was furious. Two days before campaign start, the Saltwright picked up [[Delmar-Fisk]] and [[Crisdalynn-Khinriss]] from driftwood after Umberlee's storm sank Fisk's fleet. [[Jean-Claude-Tabarnack]] boarded at campaign start by leaping onto the ship while pursued on a Midchain island.

## Current Notes

Documented cargo includes fish and shrimp in barrels on earlier runs, and salt, dried fish, and cloth in [[Session-01]]. A small gap in the aft hull panels above the waterline lets in fresh air and a sliver of outside light. [[Beaumont-Sel]]'s capuchin, Bisou, roams freely and keeps a hoard somewhere in the hold.

## Layout

The _Saltwright_ is arranged like a hard-used cargo brig with very little wasted space. The weather deck handles sail work and loading, a small aft cabin gives the captain limited privacy, and the hold does most of the real work.

### Weather Deck

Plain and unadorned, with no figurehead and no decorative railwork. It reads as a working surface for sail handling, loading, and routine Midchain passage rather than a fighting deck or prestige vessel.

### Captain's Quarters

Small and practical. [[Beaumont-Sel]] keeps a lockbox under the bed. It is one of the only spaces aboard with any real privacy.

### Hold

Roughly six feet of headroom beneath the deck beams. Cargo is stacked tight among barrels, crates, and sacks, with two lanterns hanging amidships and the hull always creaking under sail. In the aft corner, a small gap above the waterline admits fresh air and a narrow view out. Bisou's hoard is hidden somewhere in this clutter.

## Crew

[[Beaumont-Sel]] commands the ship and has done so on this route for eleven years. The full crew roster is not yet established. At least one crew member carries stank leaf in a glass jar and rolled cigarettes. Bisou, Beaumont's capuchin, has free run of the ship even if he is not crew in any formal sense.

## Operational Notes

**Roles:** Only the captain is firmly established: [[Beaumont-Sel]]. Navigator, bosun, carpenter, cook, surgeon, gunner, and full sailor complement are not yet documented.

**Living space:** Compact and cargo-first. The captain has a private cabin with a lockbox; the rest of the ship prioritizes cargo over comfort.

**Registry and reputation:** An ordinary Midchain cargo brig out of [[Kalowe]], old and salt-worked enough to disappear into normal trade traffic. No unusual registry mark, faction pennant, or special reputation is documented.
