# Ral-Arn

> _The island where memory is kept against salt and forgetting._

The record island of the Sunken Crown — larger than Dath-Mar or Meth-Var, visited when a question must be answered by memory rather than appetite. The oldest family lines maintain carved marks here: tide accounts, storm signs, records of prior seasons. Ral-Arn does not replace oral history. It anchors it. A mark here is only useful if someone living can read the story around it.

The records survive because people tend them, not because the island is gentle. [[beastiary/giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] bask across the stone paths, [[beastiary/giant-wolf-spider|giant wolf spiders]] take abandoned shelters, and [[beastiary/swarm-of-ravens|raven swarms]] gather where offerings or storm-killed fish are left too long. A visitor who cannot read the local marks can still understand the fresh claw scores.
