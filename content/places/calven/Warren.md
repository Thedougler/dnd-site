# The Warren

## Overview

The Warren is the fixed Rattkin settlement beneath [[Calveno]], built into the old drainage channels below the Saltmarsh district. Calveno grew above it. The Rattkin were already there.

Most Dry know the Warren as rumour: a place under the city bones, older than the current canals, where the [[Passage|Passage]] keeps routes, shelters, memory, and doors the surface city never meant to have. Kin speak of it with more precision and less decoration. It is home, anchor, and warning all at once.

Access is not obtained by asking. A person may be shown a door and still know almost nothing about what lies past it. Do not mark a suspected entrance. Do not boast about being invited. Do not mistake a rumour for permission.

## Districts & Landmarks

The Warren is low stone corridors and dry chambers cut into the old marsh channels, with rope bridges over flooded sections and oil lamps set into wall niches. The ceilings are low enough that most humans feel the place in their shoulders. The air smells of old stone, standing water, lamp oil, and the warmth of people living where water was supposed to go.

The sound of Calveno filters down through disguised vents: faint voices, water traffic, market noise, footsteps above. The city is always overhead, but never fully present.

## People & Power

The Warren is the most established anchor of [[Passage|the Passage]] in the Shattered Sea. Travelling Runs move between ports, holds, ships, and hidden routes, but the Warren stays. It is where memory has weight.

That does not make it public infrastructure. The Warren is not a tourist curiosity, criminal market, or shortcut for clever outsiders. It is a Kin settlement first.

## Trade & Services

The Warren provides routes, hidden shelter, memory, introductions, and kin support rather than public commerce. Its value is access, continuity, and the ability to know what moves through Calveno before surface authorities do.

## Access & Approach
