# Notable Items

Things the party owns, has encountered, or should probably be able to identify if someone points one at them.

**[[items/Clydes-Bestiary-of-Oceanic-Creatures|Clyde's Bestiary of Oceanic Creatures]]** — A naturalist's survey of oceanic creatures, written with the kind of contempt for the reader that only comes from genuine expertise. Useful for identifying things that might eat a ship.

**[[items/Pearl-of-Souls|Pearl of Souls]]** — A divine object with ties to Umberlee, Fisk's lost fleet, and whatever is at the bottom of the Maw. Nobody who's held it seems entirely comfortable talking about it afterward.

**[[items/The-Baroness|The Baroness]]** — Delmar Fisk's rapier. Well-traveled.

**[[items/The-Duchess|The Duchess]]** — Delmar Fisk's musket. Same.
