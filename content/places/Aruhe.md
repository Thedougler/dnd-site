# Aruhe

## Appearance

Aruhe is a small Midchain island immediately east of [[Verdant-Teeth#The Islands|Karath]], close enough that grung patrol boats sometimes use its reefs as cover but do not land inland. The island was once inhabited, with shallow garden terraces, a freshwater spring, and a narrow beach landing on the western shore. It has been deserted for years because nearby [[The-Grung|Grung]] activity made permanent settlement untenable.

The abandonment left the interior unobserved and unmaintained. That isolation made Aruhe a suitable place for [[raw/ingested/Blight|The Blight]] to fester. The island is now a Memorial: an unnaturally uniform overgrowth of pale flowers, swollen fungi, strangling vines, and root systems that have swallowed the old terraces.

This is an optional regional threat. The party may discover Aruhe by accident, through bad navigation, pursuit, salvage, or weather, but the Blight is not required for the main campaign plot.

## Known Facts

- **Location:** one of the Midchain islands bordering the [[Verdant-Teeth]], immediately east of [[Verdant-Teeth#The Islands|Karath]].
- **Status:** long deserted; no active settlement or formal island council.
- **Cause of abandonment:** sustained Grung danger from nearby Karath made the island unsafe to hold.
- **Current threat:** [[raw/ingested/Blight|The Blight]] has established a Memorial in the abandoned interior.
- **Campaign role:** optional boss and regional hazard; not plot-dependent.

## Overview

> [!read-aloud]
> Aruhe's beach is quiet in the wrong way. Old stone garden walls show through the undergrowth, but every line has been softened by pale flowers and mats of white fungus, and the air carries wet earth, salt, and something medicinally sweet. Past the first trees, the jungle stops varying: the same blossoms, the same vines, the same root-thick soil repeating inland as if the island has learned one shape and forgotten the rest.

## Notable Locations

**The Western Landing** — A narrow beach facing Karath's outer reefs. The old landing stones are still visible at low tide. Grung patrols can pass near enough to be seen from shore, which explains why the settlement never recovered.

**The Swallowed Terraces** — Former garden terraces now buried under fungal shelves, root mats, and pale flowering vines. These are the first signs that the island is not merely abandoned.

**The Memorial Interior** — The Blight's lair and island-wide hazard. The closer the party moves toward the Death Bloom, the more the environment acts with intent.

---

## Running Aruhe

Aruhe should feel like a wrong turn that becomes a dungeon. It can enter play if the party shelters from weather, follows a drifting wreck, avoids Karath patrols, or investigates a chart note about a deserted settlement. Nothing on Aruhe is required for Jean-Claude's Sorn arc, the Grung escalation, or the wider Shattered Sea plot.

Use [[raw/ingested/Blight|The Blight]] as the island's optional boss. Treat the whole interior as the Memorial, with the Death Bloom hidden deep enough that retreat remains possible before the encounter becomes a full boss fight.
