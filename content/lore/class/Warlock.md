# Warlock

_Class — Player's Handbook (2024)_

**Primary Ability:** Charisma · **Hit Die:** d8 · **Saves:** Wisdom, Charisma · **Armor:** Light · **Weapons:** Simple · **Spellcasting:** CHA (prepared, Pact Magic)

---

## Pact Magic

Short-rest recharging spell slots — all slots are the same level.

| Level | Slots | Slot Level | Invocations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1st | 1 |
| 2 | 2 | 1st | 3 |
| 5–10 | 2 | 3rd–5th | 5–7 |
| 11–16 | 3 | 5th | 7–9 |
| 17–20 | 4 | 5th | 9–10 |

Prepared spells scale from 2 (L1) to 15 (L20).

---

## Core Features

| Level | Feature |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pact Magic (CHA, SR recharge), Eldritch Invocations (1) |
| 2 | Magical Cunning (1 min ritual: regain up to half max slots, 1/LR) |
| 3 | Subclass |
| 9 | Contact Patron (Contact Other Plane always prepared; cast free 1/LR to speak with patron) |
| 11 | Mystic Arcanum (L6 spell, 1/LR free cast) |
| 13 | Mystic Arcanum (L7 spell) |
| 15 | Mystic Arcanum (L8 spell) |
| 17 | Mystic Arcanum (L9 spell) |
| 20 | Eldritch Master (Magical Cunning now restores ALL Pact Magic slots) |

---

## Subclasses

Archfey Patron, Celestial Patron, Fiend Patron, Great Old One Patron _(2024 PHB)_
