# The High Eyrie

![[raw/assets/banners/The-High-Eyrie.webp]]

## Overview

The High Eyrie is a 200-foot basalt sea stack at the far northeastern [[places/Crown-Islands|Crown Islands]], sheer from the waterline and salt-streaked the full height. The structures on the summit sit low and wide against constant wind — built to stay, not to impress. Pilots and fishers use it as a landmark. The only practical approach is aerial; there is nothing to grip on the rock face and no dock below. The [[factions/Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie-Summary|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] have been based here for roughly two hundred years, predating both the Dravosi and Tessarine presence in the Scatter. Three Dravosi administrators have tried to formalize arrangements with them. All three were politely declined.

What the order produces is record. From the summit, on a clear day, you can see the water change colour at the horizon where the [[places/The-Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] begins. The Sentinels have been watching that colour change, and everything around it, since before the colonial flags went up — logging compass drift, weather anomalies, surface stillness, depth sightings, wrecks, and anything else observable from circuit altitude. The ledgers are held in the main hall and are not a commercial product, but researchers who have made it up the stack have been given access. Very few try. A Sentinel will tell you what they recorded. What it means is not a question they answer.
