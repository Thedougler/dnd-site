## Backstory

Crisdalynn trained at the High Eyrie until the day Master Kyzil handed her a task: map the Drowned Maw from inside a storm. He gave it to her at fourteen because she was capable of it, and because the day before she'd been flying lazy circles when she should have been at her post. She found a berth on the _Red Lady_ as lookout and cartographer, spent two years navigating the Midchain's worst shallows, and was close to finishing when the fleet went down.

She was in the crow's nest when the fog came in — so thick the water disappeared. A rogue trench opened and all five ships plunged eighty feet into a valley of water. She dove, grabbed Admiral Fisk, and flew straight back up through closing walls of water. They heard Umberlee's laughter, then her rage, as the fleet was swallowed whole.

Two days on wreckage. Then the _Saltwright_ found them.

The charts are still in the satchel. The task is still unfinished. She hasn't gone back.
