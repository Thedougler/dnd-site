# Casa Lupo

## Overview

Casa Lupo is a general goods shop in the lower canal zone of [[Calveno#Districts & Landmarks|Le Paludi]], the oldest district in [[Calveno]]. It sells what people actually need rather than what ships need, which in Calveno makes it a distinct category.

## At a Glance

| Field | Detail |

| Known For | General goods, fair memory pricing, and the particular quiet of Le Paludi |

## Description

Low ceiling, oil lamps on iron hooks, floorboards that have been settling unevenly for a century and have reached a comfortable arrangement with gravity. The shelves go to the ceiling on three walls and there is a ladder on a rail that Ettore has not used in years because he knows where everything is without it. The smell is candle wax, dried herbs, and something faintly mineral that was probably the previous shop's business and has never quite left.

## People & Operations

**Proprietor: Ettore Lupo** — Seventy-something, moves with the energy conservation of a man who has decided he has already moved enough. Prices everything from memory and has not written a price list in decades because he does not need one and would not trust one that was not his. Will tell you clearly if he does not have something. Will not tell you where to get it unless he has formed an opinion of you, which takes a while.

## Access & Security

Street-side entrance only — no canal access. Ettore opens when he opens and closes when he closes; the hours are legible from the state of the lamp in the window. If the lamp is out, he is not in.

## Current Hooks

- Ettore Lupo keeps a sealed account of who in Le Paludi bought what and when going back forty years. He has never been asked about it and does not volunteer its existence.
