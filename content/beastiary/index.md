The [[places/Shattered-Sea|Shattered Sea]] has many things that call it home. Sailors name them, argue about them, and occasionally get killed by them. Some entries are confirmed creatures. Others are the kind of rumor that survives because the alternative explanation is worse.

## Shattered Sea Creatures

- [[beastiary/Leviathan|The Leviathan]] - rumored creature. Rumored deep-sea titan tied to the destruction of the _Vestra_. Perrin Black-Jaw survived the sinking, and his account gives the old boiling-water stories teeth. No reliable public statblock exists.
- [[beastiary/Sawek|Sawek (Blue Devil)]] - CR 5, monstrosity. Ambush predator that hunts the [[Midchain|Midchain]] blue holes, strikes with tentacles, and pulls prey down into its lair.
- [[beastiary/Whip-Shark|Whip Shark (Welak)]] - CR 6, monstrosity. Open-ocean hunter, big enough to damage a ship and equipped with a barbed tail it uses like a whip.
