# [[Harbourmasters-Office-Secrets|Harbourmaster's Office]]

A long stone building at the base of the Dockfront, counter behind a partition, two clerks with ledgers. Every vessel entering [[Port-Tidefall-Secrets|Port Tidefall]] checks in here before clearing [[Inspection-Pier-Secrets|the inspection pier]]. Crown rating certificates, impound processing, and harbour fees all run through this office.

The exterior wall carries a cork board — sun-warped, crowded with notices in various states of fading. Crown-sealed postings sit at the top. Below them, whatever else has been pinned there by whoever thought it worth pinning.

---

## Charts

The chart clerk sells official Dravosi surveys over the counter. Crown Islands and [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] charts are standard stock. Midchain coverage is partial and requires asking specifically.
