# [[Cape-Solitude-Secrets|Cape Solitude]]

The northernmost point of [[places/calders-tooth/index|Calder's Tooth]], where the island runs out of island and faces open ocean directly. Heavy surf, constant wind off the [[Galewall|Galewall]], and no shelter. Local fisherfolk won't anchor within two miles of the cape. They don't explain why if you ask them directly, but the short version is the Dead Light.

---

## The Dead Light

A ruined stone tower stands on the point — pre-colonial construction, older than the Crown's claim to the island. The Dravosi attempted a lighthouse project here and abandoned it after three successive work crews disappeared without signs of struggle. No bodies, no tools left behind, nothing disturbed. The project was quietly struck from the ledgers.

The tower still stands. Nothing has moved into it that anyone has reported, and no one has gone back to look carefully.

---

## Fauna

The surf at the cape base runs hard against a boulder field, which suits [[beastiary/beasts/giant-crab|giant crabs]] — the colony here is larger than the ones on Calder's Tooth's eastern mudflats, and more aggressive. The constant swell keeps food washing in and keeps casual visitors away from the waterline, which the crabs appear to rely on. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-vulture|Giant vultures]] use the cape's updrafts daily, circling the point in numbers that suggest they expect something to die here regularly. Given the cape's history, this is not a baseless expectation.

The locals' reluctance to anchor near the cape predates the lighthouse project. The fisherfolk say voices carry from the clifftop on windy nights — not wind noise, but voices with phrasing in them. [[beastiary/monstrosity/harpy|Harpies]] nest in cliff-face crevices across the Calder's Tooth northern headland and are known to be present on this stretch of coast. Whether the three lighthouse crews followed a sound toward the cliff edge is a question nobody has gone back to confirm.
