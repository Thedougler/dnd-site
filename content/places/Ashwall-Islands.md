# Ashwall Islands

## Overview

> [!read-aloud]
> The water goes dark long before land breaks the horizon, and the air loses the warm green smell of the Scatter. A black volcanic spire rises ahead, cold and sheer, with cloud dragging across its upper stone and white surf bursting against the base. The wind carries salt, wet ash, and the thin mineral bite of sulphur. Nothing about the island invites a landing.

---

## Lore

The Ashwall Islands are a far western island chain near the [[The-Galewall|Galewall]]. The defining landform is cold volcanic spire-rock rising directly from dark ocean, with surf-heavy approaches and sulphur vents in the high stone. They are not a current exploration destination for the party and have no active PC connection.

Ships using the western passage treat the islands as boundary markers for the Galewall approach. The local navigation culture is tied to [[Selune|Selune]], whose Ashwall worship emphasizes stars, instruments, and survival by attention rather than conquest of the sea.

## Notable Locations

### The Western Spire

The visible landmark of the chain: a cold volcanic spire standing out of the ocean near the Galewall. Cloud catches on its upper stone, surf breaks hard around its base, and sulphur vents mark the high slopes.

### Ashwall Road

The western passage route associated with Galewall crossings. Current detail is limited to its relationship with the Ashwall Islands and the northern [[Crown-Islands|Crown Islands]] trade route.

### Ashwall Primary Port

The Ashwall primary port has an active [[Selune|Selune]] temple. Current detail is limited to its religious and navigational function for Ashwall sailors.
