# Places in the Shattered Sea

Ports, islands, storm belts, routes, shrines, ruins, and dangerous water the characters can know about without crossing into GM-only material.

## Region Scale

- [[places/Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] — warm archipelago of reefs, ports, colonial claims, old ruins, sea tribute, and dangerous routes.
- [[places/Verdant-Scatter|The Verdant Scatter]] — the main lived-in archipelago between the [[places/Galewall|Galewall]] and the [[places/Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]].
- [[places/Galewall|The Galewall]] — permanent western storm belt separating the Scatter from the colonial homelands.
- [[places/Central-Strait|The Central Strait]] — main east-west shipping corridor between the [[places/Crown-Islands|Crown Islands]] and the [[places/Midchain|Midchain]].
- [[places/Doldrums|The Doldrums]] — mirror-flat dead water south of the Midchain where sail ships can lose days.
- [[places/Outer-Reach|The Outer Reach]] — open ocean beyond the Maw, thinly charted and outside ordinary colonial reach.

## Crown Islands

- [[places/Crown-Islands|The Crown Islands]] — northern arc of large, fortified islands where Dravosi authority is strongest.

### Calveno

- [[places/Calveno|Calveno]] — canal city of witnessed deals, pale stone, and carefully public commerce.
- [[places/Hook-and-Ledger|The Hook and Ledger]] — Calveno inn and practical meeting point.
- [[places/Cats-Curios|Cat's Curios]] — referral-only artificer workshop; no sign, no walk-ins.
- [[places/Warren|The Warren]] — rumoured Rattkin settlement beneath Calveno.

### Calder's Tooth

- [[places/Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]] — western gatekeeper island of the Central Strait.
- [[places/South-Harbour|South Harbour]] — eastern working harbour, less formal than Port Tidefall.
- [[places/South-Harbour-Impound|South Harbour Impound]] — Crown seizure yard on the east pier.
- [[places/Oshas-Slip|Osha's Slip]] — mudflat village and lower-profile landing near South Harbour.
- [[places/High-Line|High-Line]] — overland path and haulage route across the island.
- [[places/Overland-Track|Overland Track]] — dry route used when the official road is the problem.
- [[places/Cape-Solitude|Cape Solitude]] — wind-battered northern point of Calder's Tooth.

#### Port Tidefall

- [[places/Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]] — Dravosi fortress-port carved into the cliff face in three tiers.
- [[places/Fort-Crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] — fortified crown of Tidefall with cannon battery and command post.
- [[places/Inspection-Pier|Inspection Pier]] — first contact point for official Tidefall arrivals.
- [[places/Harbourmasters-Office|Harbourmaster's Office]] — administrative office for Tidefall berths, writs, and port paperwork.
- [[places/Governors-Seat-Tidefall|Governor's Seat]] — upper Tidefall authority and ceremonial centre.
- [[places/Tidefall-Chandlery|Tidefall Chandlery]] — port supply shop for ship goods and practical nautical gear.
- [[places/Shelf-Instrument-Dealer|Shelf Instrument Dealer]] — navigational tools: sextants, compasses, chronometers, almanacs.
- [[places/Tessarine-Trade-House-Tidefall|Tessarine Trade House]] — Concordat office handling licensing, contracts, and courier dispatch.
- [[places/Fare-Ia|Fare I'a]] — Grung-style fish jerky counter on the Dockfront.

## The Midchain

- [[places/Midchain|The Midchain]] — southern arc of close-packed islands, free ports, reefs, and local arrangements.

### Kalowe

- [[places/Kalowe|Kalowe]] — primary Midchain free port and repair harbour.

### Halythion

- [[places/Halythion|Halythion]] — sea elf place known more through rumour and fragments than easy visitation.

### Verdant Teeth

- [[places/Verdant-Teeth|The Verdant Teeth]] — Grung-held island cluster south of Kalowe.
- [[places/Sorn|Sorn]] — reef-fringed Grung island known from the water as green wall, pale sand, and danger.
- [[places/Karath|Karath]] — Verdant Teeth island with a hostile reputation and no visible invitation to land.
- [[places/Veth|Veth]] — primary gold-caste island and controlled trade beach.
- [[places/Aruhe|Aruhe]] — small Midchain island near the Verdant Teeth.
- [[places/Dreth|Dreth]] — Verdant Teeth-adjacent island in Grung waters.
- [[places/Orak|Orak]] — eastern support island in Verdant Teeth waters.

## The Tail and the Maw

- [[places/Tail|The Tail]] — eastern convergence of the Scatter near the Drowned Maw.
- [[places/Drowned-Maw|The Drowned Maw]] — vast eastern trench where charts, compasses, and sailors grow less confident.
- [[places/High-Eyrie|The High Eyrie]] — basalt sea-stack monastery of the [[factions/Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]].
- [[places/Shelfworks|The Shelfworks]] — open-water salvage field on the Maw's western shelf.
- [[places/Shelf-Instrument-Dealer|Shelf Instrument Dealer]] — salvage-market contact point for Antheri instruments.
- [[places/Antheri-Ruins|Antheri Ruins]] — ancient deep-water structures associated with the Maw and the Shelfworks.

## Beyond the Maw

### Sunken Crown

- [[places/Sunken-Crown|The Sunken Crown]] — five small islands around a vast Blue Hole in the Outer Reach.
- [[places/Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] — main settlement; tabaxi community on black basalt promontories.
- [[places/Vel-Orn|Vel-Orn]] — shrine island at the Blue Hole's centre; barren and difficult to approach.
- [[places/Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]] — the record island, where the oldest family histories are kept in stone.
- [[places/Dath-Mar|Dath-Mar]] — the refuge island, maintained for emergencies and solitary retreat.
- [[places/Meth-Var|Meth-Var]] — the watch island, facing open Maw waters.
