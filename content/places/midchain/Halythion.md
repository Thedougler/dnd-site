# [[Halythion-Secrets|Halythion]]

The name surfaces in port taverns south of the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] — usually from someone who heard it from someone else. An underwater city, old, somewhere in the eastern reefs. The reef in question is impassable by ship and not on any chart worth trusting. Vessels that have gone looking for a passage through have not returned.

Whether there is a city there or not, the reef is real. Most pilots know to route around it.

---

## Fauna

The reef that surrounds the area pilots call Halythion is dense enough to stop a ship and deep enough at its inner edge to make rescue impractical. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-shark|Giant sharks]] work the outer reef wall, drawn by the current upwellings where the deep water hits the reef structure. They are large and numerous enough that a longboat crew assessing a grounded vessel has a secondary problem in addition to the primary one.

[[beastiary/beasts/giant-octopus|Giant octopuses]] occupy the deeper reef structures — limestone overhangs, wreck debris, and any hollow structure large enough to hold them. The inner reef is known for wrecks. Wrecks accumulate octopus. Divers who have worked the outer edge of the reef's salvage opportunities note that some of the wrecks appear to have been moved.

Sailors who have passed the outer reef margin without grounding and returned describe sightings of organised shapes moving in formation through the water below the reef break — too large for reef fish, too consistent for current. The Midchain pilots who hear these accounts do not call it evidence of anything. They note the accounts and add the reef to the list of places worth routing around regardless of what is or is not down there.
