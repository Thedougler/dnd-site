# Perrin Black-Jaw

![[raw/assets/banners/Perrin-Archer.webp|Perrin, a rattkin sailor, standing on a ship deck with rope and barrels nearby]]

> _Commanded a ship to fish and support his family. Lost it to something vast coming up from below. Now hunting it._

## Overview

**Perrin Black-Jaw** — PBJ to the people who know him — is a Rattkin sailor, negotiator, and the only known survivor of whatever destroyed the _Vestra_ three days before the campaign begins. He is ten years old, squarely middle-aged by Rattkin reckoning, and by most measures he had built exactly the life he wanted: the ship, the fishing operation, the family supplied and respected. Then something came up from below the waterline on what had been an ordinary day, and now he is hiding in the hold of [[lore/Saltwright|the *Saltwright*]] with a family heirloom on his back and a conversation he is not ready to have.

He is a sleazy operator in the way a charming person with no illusions about people tends to become: manipulation, bribery, and leverage are tools he reaches for without much apology. He draws one line, firm and old: people are not cargo. Everything else has flex to it.

---

## Appearance

He travels with the hood up. What registers first is a short figure in an olive-drab cloak, three feet tall and moving with the unhurried confidence of someone who has already noted all the exits. Then the hood comes down.

Perrin is a fancy rat Rattkin — hooded colouring, which means a black head and a white body with brown patches, the pattern distinct enough that people tend to remember it. He carries a longsword at his hip and a bodhran drum on his back, which together produce an impression that is simultaneously diplomat and something that crawled in off the docks. He reads rooms the way he reads water: for what is underneath, for where the current is moving, for what is going to be useful.

He is small even by Rattkin standards in the way that matters on ships — everything physical assumes a body built for it, and his is not. He compensates with personality, and has done so for as long as he can remember.

---

## Backstory

Perrin grew up in the Tangle — the family network at the heart of [[Warren]], the only fixed Rattkin settlement in the [[Shattered-Sea|Shattered Sea]], built beneath the city of [[Calveno]]. He was raised on the practical lessons of the Run: read people, build capital, stay useful, and know which doors to use and which ledgers to stay out of. [[Dravosi-Crown|The Dravosi Crown]] keeps standing bounties on Rattkin that are rarely enforced and always present. He learned early what that kind of background pressure means for how you move through the world.

He was good at the Run's lessons. Contacts became capital, capital became crew, crew became a ship. The ship had belonged to his grandfather — [[Nona-Black-Jaw|Nona Black-Jaw's]] late husband built and captained her, then left her to the family when he died. When the _Vestra_ passed to Perrin, it came with the weight of that inheritance and with the responsibility of the route. He upgraded her, added gun mounts, crewed her himself, and ran a fishing operation.

He genuinely loved the fishing. That tends to surprise people who meet the diplomat first, but it was always the point. The contacts and the capital were how you got to the part where you could drop a line into deep water and not need to be anywhere.

---

## The Vestra

The _Vestra_ was a small two-masted brig, older than most [[Midchain|Midchain]] traffic, her topsides painted a deep green that had been reapplied so many times it no longer matched from plank to plank. A rat figurehead at the prow with one hand extended, palm up. Compact, practical, built for a small crew travelling light — everything about her read as a vessel maintained for use and personal ownership rather than prestige. She was not remarkable. She was his.

She went down three days before the campaign begins, on what had been an ordinary fishing day. The water around the hull began to bubble. Perrin touched the rail and the wood burned his hand. Then something enormous struck from below — not a reef, not a storm, something deliberate — and the hull was simply gone. The _Vestra_ went down too fast for anyone aboard to understand what was happening.

The Cloak of the Manta Ray kept him alive. He surfaced alone among wreckage. He assumed his crew was dead.

He has not told his family the full extent of what happened. They know he is alive. They do not know the ship is gone. The _Vestra_ was not just a vessel; she was the last thing his grandfather left behind, and losing her is not only a financial disaster. Perrin is staying in motion because the alternative is that conversation, and he is not ready for it yet.

---

## Keth-Naar and the Saltwright

The Cloak brought him to the surface. He washed up on the beach of [[places/Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]], and what he woke to — besides the sand and the shock — was the unfamiliar weight of a blade in his hand and the echo of something vast and distant in his head.

Perrin was not a believer before. The gods were not especially relevant to a Rattkin with fish to catch and a family to support. But something large and otherworldly found him in the water after the sinking and apparently decided he was worth speaking to. He is still working out what that means. For now it sits alongside the drum and the bestiary and the cloak as things he carries that have weight he has not fully reckoned.

He found [[lore/Saltwright|the *Saltwright*]] in the [[Keth-Naar]] harbour and tucked himself into the hold before anyone asked questions. He has made no contact with the crew. The captain does not know he is aboard.

## Character

Perrin reads people the way he reads water — for what is underneath, where the current is moving, what will be useful. This is not cruelty. It is just how he processes the world. People who affect him get managed. People who cannot do anything for him and do their job fine get left alone. He does not mistreat anyone who cannot fight back. He simply stops paying attention to them.

He is genuinely warm toward the Dry — non-Rattkin — more than he lets on. Trust at the deep level is reserved for Kin. Everyone else gets charm and read, which is not nothing, and sometimes becomes something more over time.

His one absolute is old and firm: he will not traffic people. Cultural prohibition and personal conviction both. That line does not flex. Everything else does.

When genuinely frightened — and the Dry produces enough to frighten a Rattkin who has learned to read it — his prey response comes out. He might squeak. He might cower. He finds the nearest small dark space. He is embarrassed about it afterward. A large predatory bird in the party is going to complicate this, and he already knows it.

He is not a religious man by nature, or was not. The entity that spoke to him after the sinking has complicated that. He is still figuring out what it means that a god apparently thinks he matters.

---

## The Long Game

What Perrin wants, at the bottom of it, is to go home. Run the fishing operation. Earn the respect of the elders. Become one — the old Rattkin who teaches the young ones how to move through the Dry without getting killed. The vengeance against whatever destroyed the _Vestra_ is what he tells himself gets him there.

What he has not yet noticed is that the chase has become the point, and that the version of himself who comes out the other side may not be the version the Run hierarchy will recognise as one of their own.

## Known Possessions

- **Cloak of the Manta Ray** — old, mildewy, a Black-Jaw family heirloom earned through service to the _Vestra_. Origin murky even within the Run. It kept him alive when the ship went down.
- **Longsword** — carried at the hip; not his original tool of trade.
- **Bodhran drum** — carried across the back.
- **Shield** — worn alongside the sword.
- _[[Clydes-Bestiary-of-Oceanic-Creatures|Clyde's Bestiary of Oceanic Creatures]]_ — heavily annotated since the sinking. He is learning everything he can about what lives below the waterline.

---

## Key Relationships

**[[Nona-Black-Jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]** is his grandmother, a Rattkin matriarch and senior elder of the Tangle. She is the person he is not ready to call. The _Vestra_ was her late husband's ship. Perrin knows exactly what losing it means in terms of family standing and family grief, and he is trying to fix the problem before he has to explain the loss.

**The _Vestra_** was his ship, his grandfather's inheritance, and his whole working life in a single hull. Gone. He is still carrying what it means.

**[[lore/Saltwright|The *Saltwright*]]** is where he is now, in the hold, hidden, listening to the ship work around him.

---

## Public Standing

Perrin's public reputation is small outside Rattkin circles, and inside those circles it is currently complicated. He was a competent operator with a good family name and a working ship. He still has the family name. He is also a man hiding in a cargo hold aboard someone else's boat, carrying the wreck of his grandfather's legacy and a new debt to something vast and divine that he did not ask for.

The version of Perrin Black-Jaw who comes out of what is coming will be a different thing than the one who went into the water over the Maw. He does not yet know how different.
