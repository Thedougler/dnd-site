## Overview

**Jean-Claude Tabarnack** is a blue Grung ranger, deserter, and wanderer from the Verdant Teeth, known for his scars, his directness, and a deeply serious disguise consisting of a beret and false moustache. By the opening of the Shattered Sea campaign, he is twelve years old, roughly early middle age by Grung standards, and travelling beyond the clan society that shaped him.

Jean-Claude is small even among many sailors' expectations of danger: three feet tall, amphibious, bright blue, and visibly marked by a hard life. Those who underestimate him tend to do so only once. He is most comfortable around water, vertical terrain, and darkness, where his habits as a hunter become most apparent.

## Appearance

Jean-Claude has toxic-blue Grung skin, a compact scarred frame, and the alert posture of someone who expects the room to change without warning. His scars are visible and worn without apology. He has the lean agile build common of the poison dart frog like Grung.

In human ports, he often wears a red beret and fake moustache. The choice is not a joke to him. Grung society marks caste and social role through colour, and Jean-Claude appears to have reasoned that other peoples distinguish identity through comparable visible signals, and no Grung naturally grow moustaches making his disguise perfect.

## Background

Jean-Claude comes from Grung society in the Verdant Teeth, where colour, caste, poison, slavery, and hierarchy shape daily life. As a blue Grung, he belongs to a caste associated with craft, hunting, and technical skill rather than rulership. Before his departure, he served that system as a scout and hunter.

His break with the clans is tied to Pell, a gnome slave whose friendship changed Jean-Claude's understanding of the world. Jean-Claude attempted to free him. The attempt was exposed, and Pell was killed over the following month as punishment and instruction. Jean-Claude later fled, freeing what slaves he could reach during his escape. The escape failed for many of them; Jean-Claude survived.

He is openly interested in societies built on something better than domination. His travels are not simple exile or tourism; they are a search for examples, tools, and arguments he may one day carry home.

## Desertion

Jean-Claude's desertion is both political and personal. Politically, he rejects the slave-holding order that raised him. Personally, he carries responsibility for having once participated in that order and for the deaths connected to his failed attempt to leave it behind.

This has made him less an exile than a future returnee. His stated aim is not merely to escape Grung society, but to learn enough from the wider world to return and change it.

## Abilities and Habits

Jean-Claude is a ranger trained for ambush, pursuit, and survival. He reads tracks, notices small changes, and prefers battlefields where darkness, cover, water, and height can be made to work in his favour.

He is unusually transparent in social situations. Those who know him describe him as almost incapable of hiding his intentions. This makes him a poor liar, but a clear witness: when Jean-Claude has noticed something, he usually says so plainly.

## Arrival on the Saltwright

At the opening of the campaign, Jean-Claude boards [[lore/Saltwright|the *Saltwright*]] by leaping onto the ship while being pursued on a Midchain island. The incident places him among the loose group of travellers, survivors, and fugitives converging aboard Beaumont Sel's ordinary merchant brig.

## Character

Jean-Claude is blunt, observant, and difficult to misread. He has little patience for masks he does not understand, though he has adopted one of his own with complete conviction. His instincts are those of a hunter and survivor, but his ambitions are larger than personal survival.

He wants to see the wider world, learn what other peoples have built, and eventually return with proof that Grung society does not have to remain as it is. Pell's belief that every people has unique strengths remains central to that project.

## Key Relationships

- **[[species/Grung|The Grung]]** — his people, his origin, and the society he is still measuring himself against.
- **The Verdant Teeth** — the island chain associated with Grung clans and the world he left behind.
- **Pell** — a dead gnome friend whose enslavement and death became the decisive break in Jean-Claude's life.
- **[[lore/Saltwright|The *Saltwright*]]** — merchant brig he boards at the campaign's opening.
- **[[crissdalynn-summary|Crisdalynn Khinriss]]** — fellow party member whose need for light and sky contrasts sharply with Jean-Claude's comfort in darkness and cover.

## Public Standing

Jean-Claude's public reputation is still small, but memorable. In portside retellings he is the blue Grung in the moustache, the one who appeared on the _Saltwright_ under pursuit, and the one who speaks with uncomfortable honesty about slavery, caste, and a world most outsiders know only through warnings.
