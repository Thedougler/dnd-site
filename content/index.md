_High-magic nautical sandbox. 1495 DR. Sixty thousand square miles of reef, deep channel, cliff port, and lost ruin — held together by debt, tribute, and the things sailors agree not to say out loud._

## Character Creation

**Species** — in addition to standard 2024 options, these homebrew species are available:

- [[species/Aarakocra|Aarakocra]] — winged, wind-touched, long sight
- [[species/Grung|Grung]] — amphibious, poisonous, colour-caste culture from the Verdant Teeth
- [[species/Rattkin|Rattkin]] — small, fast, built for impossible spaces
