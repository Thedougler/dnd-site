# Ashwall Islands

![[raw/assets/banners/Ashwall-Islands.webp|Dark volcanic islands rising from rough gray water beneath a stormy sky]]

## Overview

The water turns dark before the islands appear — cold, deep blue fading toward black, the warm green smell of the Scatter long gone. Then the spires rise: black volcanic stone, steep-sided, cloud dragging across the upper heights and surf bursting white against the bases. The air carries salt and something mineral beneath it, a thin sulphur bite from the vents in the high stone.

Sailors running the western passage know the Ashwalls as a threshold. Outbound, they are the last solid ground before the [[Galewall|Galewall]]. Inbound, they are the first proof of survival. Ashwall crews treat them accordingly — not as a destination, but as a reckoning point. The navigation tradition out here runs on stars and instruments and the kind of careful attention that keeps you alive when there is nothing ahead but open water.

The spires are not empty just because they are hard to land on. [[beastiary/giant-bat|Giant bats]] roost in warm vent caves, [[beastiary/giant-vulture|giant vultures]] work the cliffs after wrecks, and high-wind crews still tell [[beastiary/harpy|harpy]] stories about voices in the storm gaps. Most pilots file those stories under weather until someone follows the wrong sound inland.
