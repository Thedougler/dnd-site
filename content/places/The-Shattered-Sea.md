# The Shattered Sea

![[raw/assets/banners/The-Shattered-Sea.webp]]

## Overview

The Shattered Sea is a warm archipelago of roughly sixty thousand square miles, bounded to the west by the [[places/The-Galewall|Galewall]] — a permanent storm belt with the colonial homelands beyond it — and to the east by the [[places/The-Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], a deep-sea trench where charts stop having opinions and compass needles drift. Between them: hundreds of islands in two main arcs separated by the [[places/Central-Strait|Central Strait]]. The [[places/Crown-Islands|Crown Islands]] run the northern arc, large and heavily administered under the Dravosi flag. The [[places/The-Midchain|Midchain]] forms the southern arc, dozens of small islands packed close, largely ungoverned, running its own arrangements on local knowledge and the assumption that the colonial powers will always be slightly too slow to catch up. Warm air reaches a ship before the islands do, carrying jungle, tar, fish smoke, and wet rope.

[[Umberlee|Umberlee]] takes a fee at every harbour mouth — shrine at the entrance, posted schedule in three languages, pay and sail. The Scatter Current runs clockwise through the whole region, giving a free half-knot to ships that know where it runs. No chart of the Shattered Sea is innocent: some are wrong by age, some by politics, some because the reef moved, and some because the person who sold it had debts.
