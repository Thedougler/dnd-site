# Monk

_Class — Player's Handbook (2024)_

**Primary Ability:** Dexterity and Wisdom · **Hit Die:** d8 · **Saves:** Strength, Dexterity · **Armor:** None · **Weapons:** Simple melee + Light martial melee

AC (unarmored): 10 + DEX + WIS

## Focus Point Actions (Level 2+)

**Flurry of Blows** (1 FP) — Two bonus unarmed strikes (three at L10).

**Patient Defense** — Free: Disengage as bonus action. 1 FP: Disengage + Dodge as bonus action (+ THP at L10).

**Step of the Wind** — Free: Dash as bonus action. 1 FP: Dash + Disengage, doubled jump (+ carry ally at L10).

**Uncanny Metabolism** (1/LR) — On Initiative roll: regain all Focus Points + regain HP equal to Monk level + Martial Arts die roll.
