# The Grung

The Grung of the midchain are not a unified political entity. They are a collection of clans bound by a shared caste system that operates identically across all of them. No central authority enforces it. Every grung was raised inside it.

They occupy the interiors of a cluster of mid-sized Midchain islands — the [[Verdant-Teeth]] — roughly two days south of [[Kalowe]]. Coastal islanders trade with them, but the trade stays at the water's edge. Outsiders who have gone inland have not come back.

---

## The Caste System

Caste is determined at birth by skin colour. In rare cases a grung changes colour during their lifetime; when this happens, their caste changes with it, and the event is treated as deeply significant by the whole clan.

| Colour | Role | Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | Clan leaders, spiritual authority | Highest |
| **Red** | Scholars, memory-keepers, poison-crafters | Second |
| **Orange** | Artisans, builders, weapon-makers | Third |
| **Purple** | Warriors, hunters, law-keepers | Fourth |
| **Blue** | Scouts, messengers, handlers of slaves | Fifth |
| **Green** | Labourers | Lowest freeborn caste |

Below green: slaves, who are property, not caste. Every freeborn grung outranks every slave. Every grung outranks every non-grung. Neither is stated as a rule; both are assumed.

**Protocols** are strict. Lower castes do not initiate speech with upper castes. Lower castes do not make eye contact with gold. Red-caste grung are addressed in the plural. These protocols apply uniformly. Captives are corrected until they comply.

---

## Family and Household

Grung live in family units; clutches of two to four eggs, each hatchling's colour determining caste at birth. A single clutch can span multiple castes, which is common. A family with a gold member is spoken of differently than one without — a higher-colour hatchling is the family's achievement, not a separation. Caste protocols apply inside the household without contradiction: a blue parent addresses a purple child with full deference. The order is not a constraint on the family. It is what the family is for.

## Slavery

The grung economy runs on slave labour; the freeborn population cannot meet the society's labour demands alone. Slaves handle all work the freeborn consider beneath them: direct contact with soil, raw plant material, waste. They are branded on the right shoulder with a resin-based dye that does not fade. Slave mortality is high. The grung do not consider this a problem.

**Sources of slaves:**

- **Raid.** Night raids on neighbouring island coasts, targeting fishing settlements and isolated camps. Raiders take working-age adults. Children and the elderly are left.
- **Purchase.** Conducted at the coast through blue-caste intermediaries. Payment is in goods: preserved food, worked timber, plant materials for poison-crafting, salvaged metal and rope. Exchange happens on a designated beach on Veth's western coast. No seller comes inland.
- **Penalty.** Grung law can reduce a grung to slave status for crimes against the caste order: a lower-caste grung striking a higher, contaminating a red-caste formula, theft from a gold. Extremely rare. Deeply shameful to the offender's lineage.

**Blue caste manages slavery** — acquisition, handling, and discipline. Blue grung are the scouts who locate targets, the handlers who process purchases, and the keepers who run the slave population day-to-day. It is their caste function.

## Poison

Every grung secretes venom and is toxic to touch. This is biological, not developed.

What they have developed is a poison-crafting practice built on that baseline. Red-caste grung maintain repositories in sealed chambers in each settlement. Contents include:

- Concentrated grung secretion (baseline, available in bulk)
- Refined extracts producing paralysis
- Slow-acting variants for use on slaves (incapacitates without killing)
- Contact coatings for weapons, tools, door surfaces, and climbing holds

Purple-caste warriors coat their darts, spears, and climbing tools before operations. Blue-caste scouts use poison for capture. Green-caste labourers do not carry it but are still dangerous to touch.

**The grung do not sell, teach, or share poison formulas.**

---

## Relationship With the Midchain

Midchain islanders know the Verdant Teeth are grung territory and stay off the interiors.

The coastal trade is ongoing. Specific Midchain traders run the route to Veth's west beach regularly. The grung pay in crafted goods: carved bone, resin objects, plant pigments, alchemical specimens. These goods move through [[Kalowe]] without scrutiny. Kalowe's Council does not acknowledge where they come from.

**Raid activity has increased over the last two years.** Settlements previously out of range have been hit. The Island Council has received complaints and has not acted.

**What Midchain locals know:**

- Don't go inland on any of the Verdant Teeth.
- The west beach on Veth is where you deal. Bring what they want, don't bring extra people.
- Grung touch is poisonous.
- The raids have been getting worse. Coastal camps near the Teeth have moved back from the shore.
- To speak with a gold grung, you address a blue, not the gold directly.
