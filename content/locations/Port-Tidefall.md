# Port Tidefall

> Dravosi fortress-port carved into the cliff face of Calder's Tooth. The Crown's authority is loudest here, and most negotiable. Nothing enters the Central Strait without Dravosi say-so, and the city exists to make sure you never forget it.

**Location:** Western mouth of the Central Strait, Crown Islands

---

## Lore Sheet

Dravosi fortress-port carved into three tiers of the Calder's Tooth cliff face — Dockfront at the waterline, the Shelf mid-cliff, Fort Crestwall at the crown. Controls the western mouth of the Central Strait; nothing enters without a Crown rating inspection. The Governor answers to the Dravosi Admiralty, not locally. The three nooses at the harbour gate are a permanent fixture; nobody in port remembers when they went up.

---

## Overview

> [!read-aloud]
> The cliff comes up fast — three tiers of grey stone cut into the rock above the waterline, the fort at the crown flying a Dravosi pennant in the channel wind. The harbour mouth is narrow and the inspection pier extends to starboard before you can reach the dock. Three nooses hang from the arch above the entrance, empty, freshly painted black.

## Notable Locations

### Harbour Gate Gallows

> Stone arch at the dock entrance, wide enough for a loaded cart. Three nooses hang from the crossbeam, empty. Above the lintel, twelve tally marks cut into the stone. The gallows frame is painted black — fresh enough that you can smell it.

- **Verb:** Warn
- **Condition:** Rattkin bounty notices pinned to the arch — 15gp live, "relocation." A patrol officer who spots [[Perrin-Black-Jaw]] here has legal standing to detain on sight.
- **Link:** [[Perrin-Black-Jaw]], [[Jean-Claude-Tabarnack]] — both read this on the way in.

## Trade

Trade is ledger-driven. Garrison-sanctioned goods are straightforward; unsanctioned goods require intermediaries. Formal trade on the Shelf, informal on the Dockfront.

_Scarce:_ Tessarine goods, inland produce, arcane specialist items.
_Abundant:_ Naval gear, weapons, Dravosi charts.

### Vrtek _(Dockfront, passing through — see [[The-Anchor-and-Line]])_

Small rattkin with warm-brown fur silvering at the muzzle, coat lined with excessive pockets. Courier for [[The-Passage]], Tidefall–Kalowe route. Carries a message from [[Nona-Black-Jaw]] seeking [[Perrin-Black-Jaw]] — and, unknowingly, a crumpled note from a Crown officer's coat that describes buried treasure under the inspection pier.

- **Active Problem:** Lifted a Dravosi rating stamp from a Crown officer's coat; the officer will notice before end of watch.
- **Performance Hook:** Pockets whatever his hands land on — not theft, sorting. Produces it cheerfully when caught. Immediately picks up something else.
- **Link:** [[Perrin-Black-Jaw]] — delivers the Nona message warmly: _"She didn't sound happy, I'll tell you that."_
