### Uncommon — Faction Contacts or Specialist Traders

**[[Auto-Helm]]** — 1,400 gp. Clockwork tiller/wheel assembly that holds a set heading without a helmsman.

**[[Clockwork-Deck-Crew|Clockwork Deck Crew]]** — 2,800 gp. Four brass constructs that count as ordinary sailors for minimum crew. Standing orders only; cannot fight; require daily winding.

**[[Depth-Eye]]** — 800 gp. Hull-mounted lens. See through water to 60 ft from below-decks.

**[[Fog-Cannon]]** — 1,500 gp. Deck-mounted. 60 ft radius fog bank for 1 hour. Recharges after 24 hours.

**[[Ghost-Keel-Coating]]** — 900 gp. Advantage on Stealth checks at sea. Reapplication every 6 months.

**[[Hull-Patch-Automaton]]** — 1,600 gp. Crab-sized automaton that seals hull breaches under 1 ft automatically; reduces combat flooding loss by 1d6. Refill monthly.

**[[Sending-Stone-Anchor]]** — 1,000 gp. _Sending_ spell once per day from the ship's helm.

**[[Tide-Reader]]** — 600 gp. Current speed/direction to 20 ft depth; tidal predictions for known ports within 200 miles. Advantage on navigation checks in reef-heavy waters.

**[[Wind-Callers-Boom|Wind Caller's Boom]]** — 1,200 gp. Once per day: favourable wind for 4 hours, +30 miles/day regardless of conditions. Doesn't function in storms or the Doldrums.

---

### Rare — Catarina Da'Virelli or Senior Faction Reward

**[[Cartographers-Table|Cartographer's Table]]** — 8,000 gp. Maps the ship's route in real time. Charts are saleable (50–200 gp each). Doesn't function over the Maw below 200 ft depth.

**[[Wardstone-Figurehead]]** — 12,000 gp. Advantage on saves against magical effects. Hostile divination targeting the ship requires DC 17 spell save or fails.

_[[Arcane-Artillery]] — 3,000 gp per mount. Force-bolt cannon, no ammunition, 6 charges per short rest. (No dedicated page yet.)_

---

### Legendary — Late Campaign Only

**[[The-Drowned-Keel]]** — 25,000 gp. Ship submerges to 60 ft for up to 2 hours/day. Crew need no air during submersion. Hull becomes semi-translucent at depth.
