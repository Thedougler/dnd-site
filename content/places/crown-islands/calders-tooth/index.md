# Calder's Tooth

## Overview

Calder's Tooth rises at the western mouth of the [[Central-Strait|Central Strait]] as a massive, jagged slab of limestone and basalt — its windward face sheer and storm-battered, taking everything the [[Galewall|Galewall]] sends without complaint. The eastern side softens into mudflats, mangrove estuaries, and fishing hamlets that face the calmer Strait water. Between the two coasts runs a wet, fog-choked interior ridge riddled with sinkholes, where the roads are suggestions and the undergrowth argues back. The Crown claims the island absolutely. What that claim covers in practice is the fortifications, the harbour mouth, and the ledgers.

[[places/crown-islands/calders-tooth/port-tidefall/index|Port Tidefall]] is the island's official face — cannon on the seaward cliff, inspection pier at the harbour entrance, every ship through the Strait expected to pause and answer questions. But the island is larger than Tidefall's reach. Away from the fort guns there are villages, cliff-net hamlets, and coastline the Crown has not fully gotten around to pricing. The Strait begins here. So does the taxation.

The interior is Crown territory mostly on paper. Sinkholes and fog gullies shelter [[beastiary/beasts/giant-bat|giant bats]], root-torn ridge tracks carry [[beastiary/beasts/giant-boar|giant boar]] sign, and the open switchbacks are wide enough for [[beastiary/beasts/giant-axe-beak|giant axe beaks]] to pace wagons before striking. Villages along the wet side know to check old cisterns for [[beastiary/beasts/giant-wolf-spider|giant wolf spiders]] before sending children for water. Tidefall's walls keep out people better than they keep out the island.

## Port Tidefall

The western cliffside fortress-port is where the Crown wants incoming Strait traffic to stop first.

- [[places/crown-islands/calders-tooth/port-tidefall/index|Port Tidefall]] — Dravosi fortress-port carved into the cliff face in three tiers; full index of Dockfront, Shelf, and Crown locations inside.
- [[Fort-Crestwall|Fort Crestwall]] — fortified crown of Tidefall with cannon battery and command post.
- [[Inspection-Pier|Inspection Pier]] — first contact point for official Tidefall arrivals.
- [[Harbourmasters-Office|Harbourmaster's Office]] — administrative office for berths, ratings, charts, writs, and port paperwork.
- [[Tidefall-Chandlery|Tidefall Chandlery]] — main supply shop for ship goods and practical nautical gear.
- [[Tessarine-Trade-House|Tessarine Trade House]] — Concordat office handling licensing, contracts, courier dispatch, and salvage work.
- [[Shelf-Instrument-Dealer|Shelf Instrument Dealer]] — navigational tools, charts, almanacs, sextants, compasses, chronometers, and spyglasses.
- [[Governors-Seat|Governor's Seat]] — Tidefall administrative seat and ceremonial Crown authority.
- [[Hook-and-Ledger|Hook & Ledger]] — Shelf tavern for paperwork-adjacent business and quiet deals.
- [[Fare-Ia|Fare I'a]] — Grung-style fish jerky counter on the Dockfront.

## South & East Coast

The calmer side of Calder's Tooth is muddier, less formal, and harder for the Crown to police consistently.

- [[South-Harbour|South Harbour]] — eastern working harbour, repair sprawl, fishing fleet, and lower-profile cargo point.
- [[South-Harbour-Impound|South Harbour Impound]] — Crown seizure yard on the east pier.
- [[Oshas-Slip|Osha's Slip]] — stilt-platform fishing village over the southeastern mudflats.

## Roads & Crossings

Crossing the island means mud, fog, ridge country, tolls, and roads that behave like suggestions after bad weather.

- [[Overland-Track|Overland Track]] — muddy southern switchback road between South Harbour and Port Tidefall.
- [[High-Line|High-Line]] — northeastern cliff-face fishing hamlet built around counterweighted winches and cold yellowtail upwellings.

## Outer Points

- [[Cape-Solitude|Cape Solitude]] — wind-battered northern point with a ruined pre-colonial tower and an abandoned Crown lighthouse project.
