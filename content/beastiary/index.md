# Bestiary

The [[places/Shattered-Sea|Shattered Sea]] has things in it. Sailors name them, argue about them, and occasionally get killed by them. This is what's been confirmed.

**[[beastiary/Sawek|Sawek (Blue Devil)]]** — An ambush predator that hunts the [[places/Midchain|Midchain]] blue holes. It waits in deep water, strikes with tentacles, and pulls prey down into its lair. Most people who've seen one up close didn't come back to describe it.

**[[beastiary/Whip-Shark|Whip Shark (Welak)]]** — Open-ocean hunter. Big enough to damage a ship, fast enough that you probably won't outrun it, and equipped with a barbed tail it uses like a whip. Stays deep unless something draws it up.
