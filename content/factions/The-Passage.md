# The Passage

The collective network of traveling Rattkin communities moving between ports across the Shattered Sea. Not a nation, not a guild. A living infrastructure that predates every colonial power in the region.

---

## Structure

- **The Run** — base unit; 20–60 individuals across small vessels. Families split and join across generations. Routes recorded in scratch-notation on bone/ceramic shards, cipher-legible only to Rattkin; full circuit lives in collective memory.
- **The Hold** — Rattkin-maintained cache inside every major port's infrastructure (drainage conduits, wall cavities, dock substructure). Predates most buildings they occupy. Relocated to new foundations when cities rebuild.
- **The Tangle** — seven senior elders governing the Calveno Run (the only fixed Run). No formal authority beyond respect. Hold the Passage's most sensitive routing intelligence. See [[The-Warren]].

**Kin vs. Dry:** Rattkin (Kin) and non-Rattkin (Dry). A Dry can be a trusted partner or valued ally — still not Kin. Information that flows between Kin does not flow to Dry. Influence comes from demonstrated value: routes known, contacts held, cargo moved through cities that have never seen a Rattkin.

## Lore Sheet

The Passage does not appear on any colonial map. It predates the maps. Rattkin navigators have been running the Shattered Sea's ports for longer than any of the three powers have had ships in the water, and they will be running them after the flags change again. Every Hold is built in a foundation the city did not know it had. Every route is encoded in scratch-notation on bone and ceramic, legible only to Kin — a cipher not because it was designed as one, but because nobody thought to teach the landwalkers to read it. You cannot embargo a network you cannot see. You cannot tax a passage that leaves no record. The Rattkin do not ask permission. They remember when this was all open water and no one asked them for anything.
