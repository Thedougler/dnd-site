# Pearl of Souls

![[raw/assets/banners/Pearl-of-Souls.webp]]
A pearl the size of a closed fist. Black as deep water, with an iridescent sheen that shifts under the light in a way that never quite resolves. It is never entirely still. Anyone with a sensitivity to the dead will feel it before they see it.

The Pearl is a divine instrument of [[raw/ingested/Umberlee]]. For years it sat in one of her shrines, tended by the [[Waveservants-Secrets]], doing what it does. [[Delmar-Fisk]] took it. He did not know what it was.

What it does: it collects the souls of those who drown in its presence. Passively. Continuously. No activation, no intent required. It does not stop doing this.

[[raw/ingested/Umberlee|Umberlee]] sank his fleet in response. Five ships. All five crews. The Pearl went down with them.

---

## Where It Is

The _[[Red-Lady]]_ wreck, on the eastern edge of the [[private/places/The-Shelfworks|Shelfworks]], where the shelf drops away into the trench.

---

## Known Properties

No attunement. No activation. The Pearl draws what drowns near it and holds it. Whatever it holds cannot be recovered through ordinary means. Its history before the theft is not something the [[Waveservants|Waveservants]] discuss freely.
