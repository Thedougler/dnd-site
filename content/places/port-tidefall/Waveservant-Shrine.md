# [[Waveservant-Shrine-Secrets|Waveservant Shrine — Port Tidefall]]

The Waveservant Shrine sits cut into the cliff base at the southern end of the [[places/port-tidefall/index|Port Tidefall]] Dockfront. You hear it before you see it — tallow and salt, close and low. Inside: bare stone walls, a seawater basin at the centre, a ring of candles burning. Low ceiling.

The Waveservants operate on a tithe-and-communion model. Mariners pay before they sail or after they survive. Both are acceptable. Neither is optional. A junior attendant manages the candles and basin; a senior Waveservant manages everything else.
