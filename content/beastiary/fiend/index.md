# Fiends

Sahuagin warbands are the primary fiendish threat in the Scatter — they raid from the deep shelf and outer reef walls.

- [[beastiary/fiend/sahuagin-baron|Sahuagin Baron]] - Warlord commanding shark-riders from the deep shelf of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] and outer reef walls of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]
- [[beastiary/fiend/sahuagin-priest|Sahuagin Priest]] - Conducts blood rites at submerged shrines in the blue holes of the [[Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]]; encountered where warbands gather before a raid
- [[beastiary/fiend/sahuagin-warrior|Sahuagin Warrior]] - The raiding soldiers of the deep; encountered in packs along shelf walls and reef drop-offs
