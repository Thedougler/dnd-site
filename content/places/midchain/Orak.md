# Orak

The second of the two eastern [[Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]], sitting close enough to [[Dreth|Dreth]] that the channel between them is shadowed and flat. Orak's shore is low and wet, the roots crowding down to the waterline, the canopy running thick from the beach inward. Nothing on the beach looks guarded until the canopy moves once and then holds perfectly still.

Less militarized than [[Karath]]. Still Grung territory.

Orak's danger is low, wet, and patient. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-frog|Giant frogs]] sit half-buried along the channel edge, [[beastiary/beasts/giant-constrictor-snake|giant constrictor snakes]] hold the darker root water between Orak and [[Dreth|Dreth]], [[beastiary/beasts/giant-spider|giant spiders]] bridge the root gaps above animal paths, and [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-venomous-snakes|venomous snake swarms]] gather in warm hollows after rain. Grung scouts move through it like a known street. Outsiders do not.

---

## Fauna

Orak's threat profile runs low and patient. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-frog|Giant frogs]] sit half-buried in the channel-edge mud, using the flat water between Orak and Dreth as ambush ground for anything that comes to drink or wade. They are large enough to take a dog or a small child at the waterline, and their stillness in muddy water makes them effectively invisible until the strike is already committed.

The root systems at the Dreth channel hold [[beastiary/beasts/giant-constrictor-snake|giant constrictor snakes]] in the darker, slower sections — animals that move through water too shallow for any boat but deep enough for their body weight. Grung scouts cross this ground regularly; the path choices they make around certain root tangles document where the snakes are without explaining it to outsiders.

[[beastiary/beasts/giant-spider|Giant spiders]] bridge the gap between root systems along any trail wide enough for larger animals to use — the aerial web architecture functions as detection as much as hunting. Warm hollows after rain concentrate [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-venomous-snakes|swarms of venomous snakes]], which disperse through the undergrowth along any path warm ground creates. The interaction of these hazards is not sequential — they occupy the same ground simultaneously.
