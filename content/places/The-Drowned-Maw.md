# The Drowned Maw

![[raw/assets/banners/The-Drowned-Maw.webp]]

## Overview

The Drowned Maw is a north-south trench running along the eastern edge of the Verdant Scatter, 40 to 90 miles across. The approach is visible before any other landmark: the water changes colour from the blue-green of the Scatter shallows to something deeper and less defined, a blue with no bottom in it. The surface goes flat. Not calm — still, in a way that open ocean normally isn't. There is no current over it. Seabirds that follow ships across the Scatter turn back at the edge. No fish school over it. Compasses drift within five miles and don't recover until you're through. Most captains navigate the crossing by stars. No sounding line has found the bottom.

Ships cross the Maw routinely — it's the only route to the [[places/Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] — and most have no trouble. The ones that do have trouble come back quiet, which is its own kind of information. On the western rim, at forty feet down, there are ruins: old stone buildings, dark-alloy fittings, construction that exceeds anything currently being made. The [[places/The-Shelfworks|Shelfworks]] are the current gold rush of the Scatter, and there are salvage crews at the site most weeks. The [[places/The-High-Eyrie|Sentinels of the High Eyrie]] have been watching the Maw's surface for two centuries. [[Waveservants|The Waveservants]] dislike questions about what they think is down there, which tells you something, though not what.
