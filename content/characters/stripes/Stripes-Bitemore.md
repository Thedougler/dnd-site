## Overview

**Stripes Bitemore** is a tiger [[Tabaxi]] [[Fighter]], hunter, and current passenger aboard the [[Saltwright|Saltwright]]. His purpose is simple enough: he's been sent by [[Elder-Anton-Secrets]] to find out why the sea is angry and do something about it.

He's not subtle. He's not a diplomat. Where most sailors deal with problems by talking their way around them, Stripes looks built to stand in the middle and not move.

---

## Appearance

Tiger [[Tabaxi]] — dense, lithe, feline build, climbing agility, the physical presence of someone who can stop a charging beast with a growl. Stripes of orange and black stand out against the waters of the [[Shattered-Sea]] but disappear within its jungles.

---

## Background

Stripes comes from [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]], one of five small islands around the Blue Hole and [[Vel-Orn|Shrine Island]] at the heart of the [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown]]. His tribe has guarded an ancient shrine of [[lore/Umberlee|Umberlee]] for generations, unaware of its purpose. When the sea around the Blue Hole started feeling unusually wrathful, the elders sent their strongest hunter east to find the cause.

The tribe speaks with a distinct accent — clipped consonants, a noticable Germanic lilt that sits underneath even casual speech, a remnant from early contact with the Zhentarim and them. Stripes carries it wherever he goes. East of the [[Sunken-Crown]], it marks him immediately as someone from somewhere else.

He bartered passage aboard the _[[Saltwright|Saltwright]]_ out of [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] just days ago.

---

## Character

Stripes is a showoff. Since boarding the _[[Saltwright|Saltwright]]_ he's spent the voyage performing for the crew — climbing rigging, demonstrating shots, showing off the bracers, narrating his own maneuvers with obvious satisfaction. [[Beaumont-Sel|Beaumont Sel's]] crew are his audience now, whether they signed up for it or not.

He doesn't seem to register that being the most visible person on a small ship has costs. He also hasn't noticed there's a passenger in the hold who hasn't introduced himself.

The obligation underneath all of it is real. He's not out here for adventure or profit. [[lore/Umberlee|Umberlee]] is angry, the sea his tribe depends on is failing, and someone has to find out why.

## Key Relationships

- **[[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]]** — home island; where he shipped out from; where the elders are waiting for answers.

---

## Public Standing

Stripes doesn't have much of a reputation west of the Sunken Crown yet. What people see is a tiger tabaxi with a fighter's build, a Germanic lilt, and an apparent need to demonstrate his capabilities to anyone within sight. The [[Bracers-of-Archery|bracers]] get noticed. The accent gets noticed. The spear gets noticed.

What's underneath that — the shrine obligation, the dying blessing, the weight of what the elders asked him to do — hasn't come up yet.
