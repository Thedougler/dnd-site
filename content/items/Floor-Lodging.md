# Floor Lodging

A sleeping space on the communal floor of the [[Low-Lamp|Low Lamp]], in the warmest and most-watched room in the [[Warren|Warren]]. 1 sp per night. Safer than most places in Calveno, by a significant margin. No privacy; you will hear everything that happens in the room.
