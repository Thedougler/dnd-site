# High-Line

A fishing hamlet built into the northeastern cliffs of [[places/Calders-Tooth|Calder's Tooth]], where the cold upwellings come in thick with yellowtail and the rock face drops straight to the water. There is no beach. There are no docks. What there is: massive counterweighted wooden winches bolted to the cliff edge, lowering nets down the face and hauling the catch back up.

The settlement is vertical — ladders, rope platforms, creaking stairways cut into stone, winch-baskets that double as the only transport between levels. The fisherfolk here know every handhold and wind angle. Visitors take longer to learn which gaps are fatal.
