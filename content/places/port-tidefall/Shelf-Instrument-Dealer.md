# The Shelf Instrument Dealer

## Overview

The Shelf Instrument Dealer is a narrow [[Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]] shop for captains who need reliable navigational equipment without ordering from Calveno.

## Description

The Shelf Instrument Dealer is a narrow shopfront on the Shelf in [[Port-Tidefall|Port Tidefall]], one of the few places in the Crown Islands where a captain can buy a sextant or a proper spyglass without ordering from Calveno.

A glass-fronted case in the window holds brass instruments: sextants, compasses, and chronometers, including one with a cracked face and a reduced tag. Inside, rolled charts and printed almanacs sit on the back shelves, with a few second-hand pieces laid out on felt behind the counter.

## Function

The shop does not advertise magic, commission work, or custom devices. It sells precision equipment to people who need to know where they are, how fast they are moving, and how expensive the mistake will be if they guess wrong.

## People & Operations

The instrument dealer runs a practical counter business in charts, almanacs, and precision equipment rather than a magical or custom commission shop.

## Access & Security

The shop is open to customers with coin. Its real gate is price and the limits of official chart coverage.

## Current Hooks

- A second-hand calibrated spyglass is available for 600 gp, with provenance unknown to the seller.
- Midchain chart coverage is partial, making local knowledge more valuable than anything on the shelf.
