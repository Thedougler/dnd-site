# The Verdant Teeth

## Overview

Five closely grouped islands two days south of [[places/Kalowe|Kalowe]], rising from low reef-fringed coasts to elevated limestone ridges that disappear under dense rainforest canopy. Charts from the last thirty years mark all five interiors with some version of the same annotation. The Grung clans have held this territory as long as the Midchain has been settled, and the interiors belong to them completely. Outsiders who have gone past the tree line have not come back.

The only sanctioned point of contact is the west beach on Veth, the largest island. Blue-caste intermediaries come to the waterline while the trees remain full of unseen watchers. Trade goes through them — bring what they want, do not bring extra people, do not address gold grung directly. The grung are poisonous to touch, which is biological and not a threat display; the caste protocols matter just as much. Coastal settlements in the Midchain have noticed the raids getting worse in recent years. Veth's beach is still open for business. Leave while the canopy still sounds alive.
