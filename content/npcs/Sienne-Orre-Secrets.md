# [[Sienne-Orre|Sienne Orre]]

## Mortis Voice

**Personality in Fisk's head:** Methodical, careful, and older than she had any business being on a ship running those routes. Keeps notes on everything. Reads contracts twice. When Sienne surfaces, Delmar slows down and wants more information before committing. He asks questions that look like stalling. They are not — she is building a picture and needs one more piece.

**Forced trigger:** Delmar becomes the target of a spell of 2nd level or higher, or fails an Intelligence saving throw. Sienne surfaces when the unknown intrudes.

**Environmental trigger:** A sign, figurehead, carving, or painting depicting a green mermaid — particularly on or near a harbor establishment. Sienne surfaces with a quiet recognition that reads as wistfulness, which sits wrong on Delmar's face. He may ask about the place by name, or ask how long it's been there, or simply stand looking at it a moment longer than makes sense. There's nothing urgent about it. That's what makes it noticeable.

**Skills granted:** Arcana, Investigation

**Roleplay cue:** Delmar starts asking questions instead of answering them. Wants to understand what just happened before deciding what to do about it. The charm quiets — almost professorial.
