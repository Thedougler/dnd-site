# The Red Flat

Dark [[Rum|rum]], fresh pig blood, and lime over a wooden cup — not written on any board at the [[The-Flat-Water|Flat Water]], but [[Reweti-Kahurangi|Reweti]] keeps a small clay pot of blood behind the bar specifically because the [[Five-Blades|Five Blades]] are regulars. 8 cp. She will make it for anyone who asks correctly. What "correctly" means is apparent from context.
