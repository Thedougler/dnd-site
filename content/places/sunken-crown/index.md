# The Sunken Crown

The Sunken Crown is a small [[places/Outer-Reach|Outer Reach]] island region: five black-stone islands around a vast Blue Hole where turquoise shallows drop suddenly into deep indigo. It sits beyond the [[places/Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]], where colonial charts thin out and local water knowledge matters more than official paper.

## Region Overview

- [[Sunken-Crown|The Sunken Crown]] — public gazetteer entry for the region as a whole.
- [[places/Outer-Reach|The Outer Reach]] — open ocean beyond the Drowned Maw.
- [[places/Drowned-Maw|The Drowned Maw]] — eastern trench crossed before reaching these waters.
- [[places/Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] — wider archipelago of reefs, ports, colonial claims, and dangerous routes.

## The Five Islands

Each island has a role in the life of the Sunken Crown. Keth-Naar is the main settlement; the others are used for shrine-keeping, records, refuge, and watch duty rather than ordinary expansion.

- [[Keth-Naar|Keth-Naar]] — main tabaxi settlement on black basalt promontories, with tide pools, hunting routes, and the clearest point of contact for visitors.
- [[Vel-Orn|Vel-Orn]] — barren shrine island at the Blue Hole's centre, kept apart from ordinary travel.
- [[Ral-Arn|Ral-Arn]] — memory island where family lines, tide accounts, storm signs, and seasonal records are kept in stone.
- [[Dath-Mar|Dath-Mar]] — refuge island for emergencies, hard weather, and solitary decisions.
- [[Meth-Var|Meth-Var]] — eastern watch island facing open Maw waters, staffed by rotating Keth-Naar watchers.

## Shrine And Water-Debt

- [[Umberlee-Shrine|Umberlee's Shrine — Sunken Crown]] — old shrine cut into black stone on Vel-Orn, tied to drowned names, offerings, and water-debt.
- [[lore/Umberlee|Umberlee]] — deity associated with the sea's debts and what the water takes.
- [[Waveservants|Waveservants]] — shrine-keepers and sea-priests associated with Umberlee's local worship.

## Access Notes

Reaching the Sunken Crown means crossing east of the Maw into the Outer Reach, then finding the reef channels around the Blue Hole. The region is not closed, but it is not casual water.

- The Blue Hole dominates local navigation; the edge between safe shallows and the drop is abrupt.
- Reefs and channels around the five islands do not behave consistently enough for outside charts to settle the question.
- Keth-Naar visitors are watched before they are welcomed.
- Vel-Orn has no friendly harbour and is approached only with care, restraint, and local guidance.
- Meth-Var faces the first signs from open Maw waters; warnings from there matter.

## Hazards

- [[beastiary/giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] — common on black stone after storms and rain.
- [[beastiary/giant-bat|Giant bats]] — roost in wind-cut caves and exposed cracks.
- [[beastiary/giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]] — nest where old paths and shelters fall out of use.
- [[beastiary/giant-vulture|Giant vultures]] — wait out weather on exposed lee rocks around Meth-Var.
- [[beastiary/swarm-of-ravens|Raven swarms]] — gather where offerings, storm-killed fish, or wreckage are left too long.
- [[beastiary/reef-shark|Reef sharks]] — patrol the shallows around the Blue Hole's edge.
- [[beastiary/giant-octopus|Giant octopuses]] — use cracks and drop-off shelter near the Blue Hole.
- [[beastiary/Sawek|Sawek]] — a name outsiders use too freely for large blue-hole shadows; local watchers are more careful.
- [[beastiary/dragon-turtle|Dragon turtles]] — rumoured in the farther Outer Reach.
- [[beastiary/harpy|Harpies]] — possible calls are marked separately from wind noise by Meth-Var watchers.
