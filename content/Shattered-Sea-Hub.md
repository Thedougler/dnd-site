# The Shattered Sea — Campaign Hub

> [!abstract] Campaign Brief
> **System:** D\&D 5e (2024) · **GM:** Nick · **Setting:** [[The-Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] · **Status:** Session 1 prep complete
>
> Sixty thousand square miles of colonial contest, reef, and deep water. Three powers failing to govern it. Indigenous islanders who predate them. At the eastern edge, a planar fissure is widening beneath the [[The-Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]] — and something already came through it. The last guardian just woke up. The players don't know any of this yet.
