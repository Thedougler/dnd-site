# [[Rael-Corven|Rael Corven]]

## Mortis Voice

**Personality in Fisk's head:** Steady, commanding, protective. Maternal and hard in equal measure. When Rael surfaces, Delmar stops performing and starts moving people — less charm, more weight. He doesn't manage the room; he fills it with authority. Speaks less than Delmar, and what he says lands differently.

**Forced trigger:** An ally drops to 0 HP within 30 feet. Rael's instinct is crew protection — he takes the wheel when one of his people goes down.

**Skills granted:** Intimidation, History

**Roleplay cue:** Delmar stops smiling and starts commanding. Warmth is still there; it just stops leading.

**Note:** Rael is the loudest voice of the five — he was inside the shrine cave with Fisk when the fleet sank. His trigger (ally at 0 HP) also fires most frequently in combat, making him the most disruptive captain in a fight.

## DM Notes

**Navy hatred** — Rael's father was a merchant sailor killed when the navy sank his vessel during an action against privateers. The navy classified the ship as a pirate affiliate to cover their seizure of the cargo. Rael knows this was a lie. He has generalized the resentment to the institution entire. In play this means any naval officer, patrol vessel, or Crown enforcement presence can raise his hackles — if Delmar is dealing with a naval figure and Rael surfaces, the scene shifts. He doesn't negotiate with uniforms. If the party ever interacts with Dravosi Crown naval authority, this is a live trigger even without an ally dropping.

**Father's philosophy as Mortis texture** — "Prepare for the worst, work toward the best." When Rael surfaces in Fisk's body, he's not just commanding — he's already scanned for what can go wrong and begun accounting for it. Where Bastian makes scenes and Detto states facts, Rael issues positions that already have the contingency baked in. His calm is not passivity; it's preparation that's already been done.

**Cloak of Useful Things** — Rael was known to carry one. It went down with him on the _Red Lady_. The wreck sits at the Shelfworks' eastern shelf descent alongside the Pearl of Souls. The cloak is recoverable if the party reaches the wreck site — likely intact given the magical nature of the item, though condition is at DM discretion.
