# Karath

## Overview

Karath looks like the other [[places/Verdant-Teeth|Verdant Teeth]] from the water — low reef approaches, rainforest running close to the shore, no harbour, no smoke, no visible reason to stop. The distinction is what happens when you get close anyway. Outsiders who nose into the reef gaps are met with dart fire, no warning, no gap between the quiet beach and the response. Pilots who know these waters treat Karath specifically as the island you give extra room, not because the others are inviting but because Karath is the one with a reputation for not waiting to see what you were planning.

[[places/The-Midchain|The Midchain]] coastal settlements have noticed the raids out of this direction have been getting worse. The Island Council has received the complaints. Whether either of those facts will produce consequences is an open question the people living near the water are watching closely.
