# Places in the Shattered Sea

Ports, islands, storm belts, routes, shrines, ruins, and dangerous water the characters can know about without crossing into GM-only material.

## Region Scale

- [[places/Shattered-Sea|The Shattered Sea]] — warm archipelago of reefs, ports, colonial claims, old ruins, sea tribute, and dangerous routes.
- [[places/Verdant-Scatter|The Verdant Scatter]] — the main lived-in archipelago between the [[places/Galewall|Galewall]] and the [[places/Drowned-Maw|Drowned Maw]].
- [[places/Galewall|The Galewall]] — permanent western storm belt separating the Scatter from the colonial homelands.
- [[places/Central-Strait|The Central Strait]] — main east-west shipping corridor between the [[places/Crown-Islands|Crown Islands]] and the [[Midchain|Midchain]].
- [[places/Doldrums|The Doldrums]] — mirror-flat dead water south of the Midchain where sail ships can lose days.
- [[places/Outer-Reach|The Outer Reach]] — open ocean beyond the Maw, thinly charted and outside ordinary colonial reach.

## Crown Islands

- [[places/Crown-Islands|The Crown Islands]] — northern arc of large, fortified islands where Dravosi authority is strongest.
- [[places/calders-tooth/index|Calder's Tooth Index]] — island-level index for Calder's Tooth, Port Tidefall, South Harbour, overland crossings, and nearby hazards.

### Calveno

- [[places/Calveno|Calveno]] — canal city of witnessed deals, pale stone, and carefully public commerce.
- [[Hook-and-Ledger|The Hook and Ledger]] — Calveno inn and practical meeting point.
- [[places/Cats-Curios|Cat's Curios]] — referral-only artificer workshop; no sign, no walk-ins.
- [[places/Warren|The Warren]] — rumoured Rattkin settlement beneath Calveno.

## The Midchain

- [[Midchain|The Midchain]] — southern arc of close-packed islands, free ports, reefs, and local arrangements.
- [[places/midchain/index|Midchain Index]] — regional index for Kalowe, independent waters, the Verdant Teeth, eastern reefs, passage notes, and hazards.
- [[Halythion|Halythion]] — sea elf place known more through rumour and fragments than easy visitation.

## The Tail and the Maw

- [[places/Tail|The Tail]] — eastern convergence of the Scatter near the Drowned Maw.
- [[places/Drowned-Maw|The Drowned Maw]] — vast eastern trench where charts, compasses, and sailors grow less confident.
- [[places/High-Eyrie|The High Eyrie]] — basalt sea-stack monastery of the [[factions/Sentinels-Of-The-Eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]].
- [[places/Shelfworks|The Shelfworks]] — open-water salvage field on the Maw's western shelf.
- [[Shelf-Instrument-Dealer|Shelf Instrument Dealer]] — salvage-market contact point for Antheri instruments.
- [[places/Antheri-Ruins|Antheri Ruins]] — ancient deep-water structures associated with the Maw and the Shelfworks.

## Beyond the Maw

- [[Sunken-Crown|The Sunken Crown]] — five small islands around a vast Blue Hole in the Outer Reach.
- [[places/sunken-crown/index|Sunken Crown Index]] — regional index for Keth-Naar, the five islands, Vel-Orn's shrine, access notes, and hazards.
