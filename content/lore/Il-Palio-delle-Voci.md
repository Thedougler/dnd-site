# Il Palio delle Voci Contese

> _The contest of the warring voices. Every year Calveno remembers it cannot decide what it loves, and makes the music fight for it._

## Overview

**Il Palio delle Voci Contese** — the Grand Contest of the Warring Voices — is Calveno's open bardic competition, held across three nights in the canal district each year. Multiple bands claim stages simultaneously across the Mercatura, the Velo Quarter, and the Le Paludi waterfront. The crowd is the judge: it moves freely between stages all evening, and whichever band draws the densest sustained crowd through the Grande Finale wins the Palio banner and the purse.

There are no judges. There is no panel. The crowd votes with its feet, continuously, in real time. A band that peaks too early loses the audience to whoever is building slower. A band that never quite gets there loses to whoever is peaking at the right moment. The tactics are as much theatrical as musical.

Any group may enter. Entry is free. The competition organisers — a rotating committee of Mercatura merchant families — care about spectacle, not pedigree.

## The Stages

Three stages are constructed each year, each with its own character:

**Il Palco della Mercatura** — The primary stage, built on the wide plaza at the main canal bridge. Largest capacity. Worst acoustics. The crowd here is merchants, factors, and officers who came because it is visible and stayed because they were seen.

**Il Palco del Velo** — The Velo Quarter stage, set up across the low bridges where Calveno stops enforcing its rules. Smaller, louder, unpredictable. The crowd here came specifically for this and has opinions.

**Il Palco delle Acque** — The waterfront stage on the Le Paludi canal dock, torchlit, half the audience arriving and departing by boat. The acoustic carry over the water is remarkable. This stage favours volume and visual drama.

Bands are assigned a home stage by draw, but may play off-stage busking and crowd work between sets to pull audience from other stages.

## The Competition Structure

**La Prova** _(The Trial — first night)_ — All bands play short opening sets. No elimination, but the crowd forms initial impressions that are difficult to shift. The band that dominates La Prova does not always win, but the band that embarrasses itself here rarely recovers.

**La Sfida** _(The Challenge — second night)_ — Full sets, rivalries visible. Crowd movement between stages accelerates. Bands may interact — a show of solidarity, a deliberate clash of schedules, a stunt that upstages someone else's set. The organisers look the other way.

**Il Grande Finale** _(The final night)_ — All bands play simultaneously, maximum effort, for three hours. The winning band is the one with the crowd at midnight.

## Entering

Any group that shows up at the Mercatura committee office three days before La Prova can register. The committee asks for a band name and a home stage preference. It does not ask for references.

Groups without a full complement can recruit additional performers in Calveno before registration closes. The city has musicians, and musicians who want a shot at the Palio without the risk of leading their own band are findable at the Ponte Bassa most evenings.

Roles in a Palio band are not limited to performers. Stage crew — riggers, pyrotechnic handlers, lighting, dramatic prop work — contributes materially to a band's impact. Crowd work between sets is legal and effective. The Palio is won by all of this together, not just the music.

## The Prize

The Palio itself is a painted banner, commissioned fresh each year, depicting something the organisers consider representative of Calveno's character. Winning it carries the kind of social weight in Calveno that money cannot directly buy. The purse is 300 gp, split among registered band members.

Past winners are commemorated on a plaque at the Mercatura committee offices. The plaque is visible from the street. Calveno takes this seriously.

## Current Competitors

**La Canzone Nera** _(The Black Song)_ — Four human musicians from the interior, technically flawless. Their set is precise, their stage is immaculate, and they are completely boring. Favoured by the Council families. They win La Prova on execution and then watch their crowd erode over two nights.

**Il Vento di Seta** _(The Silk Wind)_ — A halfling trio with a Tessarine booking agent and a crowd that already knows all the words. They play the same set every year, perfectly, and the crowd that loves them is devoted and returns exactly to the same size. They have placed second three times.

**Le Ossa del Toro** _(The Bones of the Bull)_ — An orc and tortle two-piece using drums and a massive horn. Loud, physical, no finesse. Their crowd surges and compresses and occasionally knocks people into the canal. They have never placed higher than third. They have never cared.

## Current Hooks

- Registration closes in three days. The committee office is on the Mercatura, two buildings from the Tessarine factor house.
- A halfling fiddle player at the Ponte Bassa has been watching the door every night this week, waiting for someone to ask him if he's available.
- Prospero Morsani has attended every Palio for as long as anyone can remember. He has never said who he was rooting for. He usually appears at the winning stage just before midnight, which the crowd has started to read as an omen.
