# The Midchain

![[raw/assets/banners/The-Midchain.webp]]

## Overview

The Midchain is the southern arc of the Verdant Scatter: dozens of limestone and coral islands packed close, most of them a half-day's sail from the next, with the treeline of one island appearing before the last one drops below the horizon. The water shifts from turquoise over the shallows to blue-black where the shelf drops away and the line between them is abrupt. Dense rainforest runs to the water's edge on most coasts; on others, pale limestone cliffs drop straight to the reef. Freshwater springs are common. Charts for the region contradict each other — reefs shift, channels silt, and local knowledge is worth more than paper in most of the back channels.

The colonial powers hold strategic points and run patrol cutters through the lanes, but three centuries of trying has not produced anything resembling full administration. Most of the Midchain belongs to island councils, free ports, pilot families, market bosses, and anyone who knows the reef better than whoever is chasing them. [[places/Kalowe|Kalowe]] is the primary settlement — the only Tier 2 dry dock outside colonial control — and the usual entry point for unregistered vessels and cargo that cannot clear Strait inspection. Each island beyond it has its own arrangements, its own welcome, its own price, and its own reef.
