# Ral-Arn

> _The island where memory is kept against salt and forgetting._

The record island of the Sunken Crown — larger than Dath-Mar or [[Meth-Var]], visited when a question must be answered by memory rather than appetite. The oldest family lines maintain carved marks here: tide accounts, storm signs, records of prior seasons. Ral-Arn does not replace oral history. It anchors it. A mark here is only useful if someone living can read the story around it.

The records survive because people tend them, not because the island is gentle. [[beastiary/beasts/giant-lizard|Giant lizards]] bask across the stone paths, [[beastiary/beasts/giant-wolf-spider|giant wolf spiders]] take abandoned shelters, and [[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-ravens|raven swarms]] gather where offerings or storm-killed fish are left too long. A visitor who cannot read the local marks can still understand the fresh claw scores.

---

## Fauna

The stone path network across the carved record surfaces is [[beastiary/beasts/giant-lizard|giant lizard]] territory — the flat, exposed rock holds heat well into the evening, and the lizard population is large enough that visitors to the record panels routinely wait for an animal to clear a section before working it. They do not attack unless cornered, but they are large enough to cause serious problems if surprised on a narrow path above the waterline.

[[beastiary/beasts/giant-wolf-spider|Giant wolf spiders]] have taken the abandoned shelters — the structures that Keth-Naar families no longer maintain are warm, dark, and undisturbed, which suits wolf spiders precisely. Any shelter that has gone unvisited for more than a season should be checked before it is used. The shelters that have been unvisited for years are checked with a pole.

[[beastiary/beasts/swarm-of-ravens|Swarms of ravens]] gather wherever offerings or storm-killed fish have been left too long. The birds are aggressive in numbers and have learned that arrival of people on the island correlates with food. They do not distinguish between offerings, stored provisions, and things left in the open during a work break.
