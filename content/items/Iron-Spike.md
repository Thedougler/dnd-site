# Iron Spike

Heavy iron spikes for fastening deck planks, hull repairs, and general construction. Sold in lots of 10. 1 gp per 10 at dry-dock ironmongers.

**Properties \[RAW]:** Can be driven into wood or stone surfaces to anchor rope, block doors, or jam mechanisms. Drive into surface: Action.
