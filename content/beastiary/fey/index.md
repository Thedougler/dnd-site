# Fey

Creatures with ties to the Feywild. Goblins are the primary fey threat encountered in the Scatter's interior islands.

- [[beastiary/fey/goblin-boss|Goblin Boss]] - Commands a goblin warband; smarter and more dangerous than the warriors it leads
- [[beastiary/fey/goblin-warrior|Goblin Warrior]] - Standard goblin raider; dangerous in numbers, quick to flee when the odds shift
- [[beastiary/fey/goblin-minion|Goblin Minion]] - The weakest goblin variant; used as fodder and distraction
