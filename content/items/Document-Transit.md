# Document Transit

Secure transit for sealed documents through the [[Passage|Passage's]] Kin-network logistics. Available from [[Marrow|Marrow]] at [[Marrows|Marrow's]] in the [[Warren|Warren]]. 5 gp minimum. Price reflects content sensitivity, not physical weight. No questions asked. Three-week consignment period; unclaimed items resolved on Marrow's terms.
