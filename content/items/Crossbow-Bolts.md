# Crossbow Bolts

Iron-tipped wooden quarrels for [[Light-Crossbow|light]] and [[Heavy-Crossbow|heavy]] crossbows. Sold in lots of 20. 1 gp per 20. Standard ammunition stock at most ironmongers with arms on the back shelf.
