## Overview

Nona Black-Jaw is a Rattkin elder and the head of the Black-Jaw Run — the most senior voice in [[Warren]], the fixed Rattkin settlement beneath [[places/calveno/index]], and a central figure in [[Passage|The Passage]] network that runs through every port in the Scatter. She is [[private/players/Perrin-Black-Jaw|Perrin's]] grandmother. She is the person he is not ready to call.

Her late husband built the _[[Vestra|Vestra]]_, captained her across the [[places/midchain/index|Midchain]] for decades, and left her to the family when he died. The ship passed to Perrin. She has not heard from him since. She has sent messages through the Passage asking him to make contact. He has not responded.

## Appearance

Very small, even by Rattkin measure. White fur from snout to tail-tip — the fur along her jaw and lower muzzle runs dark brown, almost black, which is where the name comes from. Her eyes are sharp and pink-rimmed, set deep under a low brow. The knuckles of her hands are prominent, the fur around her ears thin with age. None of this stoops her. She sits perfectly straight, hands folded, nothing hunched about her.

She dresses plainly — dark layered cloth, no adornment. Her tail rests coiled at her feet.

## Manner

She feeds people first. Everyone who comes through her door gets called thin and offered food, and if you refuse you will hear about it warmly and at length. By the time terms are on the table you have already eaten, which is the point.

She discusses Dravosi colonial policy, burning things down, and trade routes in the same comfortable tone she uses for the weather. She has been at the centre of the Tangle long enough that urgency stopped impressing her some time ago. The warmth is real. The steel underneath it is also real, and the two are not in conflict.

## The Vestra

Her late husband built the ship, captained her for decades, and left her to the family when he died. Perrin crewed her, upgraded her, and ran a fishing operation on her for years. She is gone now. Nona does not yet know the full shape of that loss. Perrin knows exactly what telling her will cost, and he is running out of time before the silence starts answering for him.
